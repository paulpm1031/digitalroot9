# Operational Bottleneck Audit: The Rosewood Clinic

**Analysis Type:** Patient Leakage & Process Friction Assessment
**Methodology:** Lean Six Sigma (DMAIC) / Constraint Theory review of scraped web assets, booking UI, and patient feedback signals
**Facility:** The Rosewood Clinic — Functional Medicine, TTDI, Kuala Lumpur

---

## 1. Key Findings

### 🔴 Booking Friction: **CONFIRMED — SEVERE (Critical Constraint)**

The "Make an appointment" section is the single largest leak point in the funnel. Evidence from the scrape:

| Evidence | Operational Implication |
|---|---|
| The booking widget renders a **Cloudflare Turnstile CAPTCHA** ("Checking your Browser… Verifying…") that cycles through **four distinct failure states** in the scraped DOM: *Verification failed*, *Verification expired*, *Refresh*, *Troubleshoot* | The scraper — and by extension, real users on restrictive networks, older browsers, privacy extensions, or in-app browsers (Instagram/Facebook webviews common in MY mobile traffic) — is being **blocked at the point of highest intent**. Every expired/failed verification is a lost patient. |
| No visible **real-time calendar or slot selector** is exposed in the scrape — only a dropdown to "Select Doctor" with 6 practitioners (Dr Iman Thalia, Dr Iman Lim, Dr Krishnaveni, Dr Jevan, Prof. Rahmattulah, Shelagh MacFarlane, Dr Lim Kheng Yee) | Patients must commit to a *provider* before seeing *availability*. No open-slot transparency = abandonment to competitors with instant booking. |
| Contact channels are limited to **Tel: +60 3 4810 6037** and **WhatsApp: +60 13 575 8121** — both are **reactive, synchronous, single-threaded** channels | With 7 doctors and a premium functional-medicine price point, a single WhatsApp line and one landline cannot triage demand. Inquiries during consults = missed calls = leaked leads. |

**Verdict:** High-intent patients are being filtered out by a bot-mitigation layer before they ever reach a booking form. This is not a marketing problem — it is a **conversion-path defect**.

---

### 🔴 Administrative Gaps: **CONFIRMED — HIGH SEVERITY**

The clinic explicitly documents its own 4-step process, which reveals a **manual, front-loaded intake burden**:

> *Step 01 — Data Gathering: "a detailed patient intake questionnaire, biometric measurements, and a review (if applicable) of past patient medical records, and blood tests."*
> *Step 02 — Consultation & Tests: "The **1+ hour** consultation…"*

Analysis:

- **No evidence of a pre-visit digital intake link** sent post-booking. The phrasing "detailed patient intake questionnaire" and "biometric measurements" implies this is collected **on arrival**, consuming paid clinician minutes and waiting-room capacity.
- A 1+ hour consult slot × 7 doctors on a **26-hour/week operating window** (Mon 9–1, Tue–Sat 9–5) means **every minute of duplicated data entry directly cannibalizes billable consult capacity**.
- **Medical records + blood tests "reviewed if applicable"** suggests staff are manually chasing and digitizing external PDFs/lab reports — classic duplicated-entry waste (Lean's *DOWNTIME*: Defects, Over-processing, Waiting).

**Verdict:** The clinic is running a **manual intake pipeline against a premium, high-touch service model**. This caps throughput and forces the front desk into data-entry roles instead of patient experience roles — a constraint that will worsen with volume growth.

---

### 🔴 Communication Failures: **CONFIRMED — MODERATE-HIGH SEVERITY**

Two structural signals stand out in the scrape:

1. **Sunday: CLOSED** (and Mon only half-day) with **no evidence of automated after-hours capture mechanism** — no chatbot, no auto-responder flow, no "we'll reply by X" acknowledgment surfaced. Every weekend inquiry via WhatsApp/Tel goes into a silent void until Tuesday 9 AM.
2. **Zero structured post-consultation retention loop is visible.** Step 04 ("Evaluation & Review… monitor, review, and optimise") is described as a *clinical* stage — not an *automated patient-engagement* one. There is no mention of recall reminders, re-test nudges, or re-engagement triggers.

Notably, the patient reviews — while glowing (Amelia, Ángel, aini ali, Fatin, Jeffrey, Priti, sarahbasir, Vinishyaa, Diana) and unanimous on clinician quality — **mention nothing about reminders, follow-up communications, or digital convenience**. No review praises nor complains about scheduling follow-ups, which strongly suggests follow-ups are handled ad-hoc and inconsistently. For a clinic whose business model depends on **repeat lab panels, protocol adjustments, and long-term protocol adherence**, the absence of an automated retention engine is a serious revenue leak.

**Verdict:** The clinic has **excellent clinical NPS** but **no operational system to monetize loyalty**. Reviews prove the product is great; the leak is in the lifecycle *after* the consult.

### Proposed Engagement: Three Custom n8n Workflows

We will engineer three interlocking, custom **n8n** automation workflows, integrated with your existing website, WhatsApp Business, and practice management stack.

#### **Workflow 1 — Frictionless Frontend Booking**
> *Targets: Booking Friction (Critical)*

#### **Workflow 2 — Pre-Consult Digital Intake Pipeline**

**Outcome:** Converts a manual, error-prone, waiting-room process into a **zero-touch digital pipeline**. Returns an estimated 15–25 minutes of clinician time per consult — the equivalent of 1–2 additional billable appointments per doctor per week.

#### **Workflow 3 — Lifecycle Retention & After-Hours Capture**
> *Targets: Communication Failures (Moderate-High)*

- **Automated appointment reminders**: WhatsApp + SMS at T-72h, T-24h, and T-2h with one-tap reschedule.
- **Structured post-consultation loop**: T+1 day (care check-in), T+7 days (protocol adherence nudge), T+30/60/90 days (lab re-test & follow-up rec booking triggers).
- **After-hours auto-responder**: Inquiries landing Sunday or after 5 PM receive an immediate branded acknowledgment with a self-serve booking link — capturing lead intent while the clinic is dark.

**Outcome:** Converts a one-time consultation into a **managed patient lifecycle**, protecting future revenue and reducing no-shows.
