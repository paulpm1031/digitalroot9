**1. Booking Friction — CONFIRMED (Severe)**
- The "Make an appointment" flow relies on a Cloudflare Turnstile verification widget that visibly fails on-page: "Stuck? Troubleshoot," "Verification failed," "Verification expired" (×2). Every failure state is a patient hitting a wall with no fallback.

**2. Administrative Gaps — CONFIRMED (Visible in the process itself)**
- Step 01 of the clinic's own 4-step process is "a detailed patient intake questionnaire." For a functional medicine clinic offering metabolic panels, genetic testing, and gut health assessments, a paper or PDF intake is a massive drop-off point between booking and consult.
- No intake link, patient portal, or pre-visit automation is visible anywhere in the funnel.

**3. Communication Failures — LIKELY (Systemic risk, not yet review-visible)**
- The 4-step process ends at "Evaluation & Review: We monitor, review, and optimise." There is no evidence of an automated recall/nudge system to make step 04 actually happen. Reviews are glowing — which means the risk isn't dissatisfaction, it's *silent attrition*: patients who never get rebooked for follow-ups and simply fade away.

**2. Digital Intake Automation**
- Conditional n8n flow: booking confirmed → patient receives a personalized intake form link (branching by service: metabolic panel, genetic testing, gut health, general consult).
- Responses auto-populate a structured pre-consult brief for the doctor, tagged to their calendar slot — so the 1+ hour consultation starts with data, not paperwork.

**3. Post-Consult Review & Recall Engine**
- n8n workflow triggered by treatment plan completion: automated check-in at Day 7, Day 30, and Day 90.
- If patient reports symptoms unresolved → routes to doctor's queue for review.
- If patient reports success → triggers review request (Google) and rebooking prompt for the next milestone assessment.
- This directly closes the loop on your Step 04 "Evaluation & Review," which today relies entirely on patients remembering to come back.
