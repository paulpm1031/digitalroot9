## 1. Key Findings

### 🔴 Booking Friction — **CONFIRMED: SEVERE**

**Evidence:**

The "Make an appointment" section contains a dropdown selector ("Select Doctor: Dr Iman Thalia / Dr Iman Lim / Dr Krishnaveni Kanason / Dr Jevan Gunasenalan / Professor Dr Rahmattulah Khan / Shelagh MacFarlane / Dr Lim Kheng Yee") but **no visible real-time calendar, no slot availability, no instant confirmation, and no date/time picker in the scraped DOM**. Combined with the embedded **Cloudflare Turnstile challenge**, the evidence points to a booking flow where the patient selects a doctor → hits a bot-verification wall → and is then routed to an unconfirmed path (likely a manual callback or WhatsApp handoff).

The Turnstile block is particularly damning because the scraped text captured **multiple failure states**: "Checking your Browser…", "Verifying...", "Stuck? Troubleshoot", "Verification failed", "Verification expired – Refresh". A patient seeing *"Verification expired"* mid-booking is a textbook drop-off trigger.

Compounding this: the only contact channels listed are **Tel: +60 3 4810 6037** and **WhatsApp: +60 13 575 8121** — no online booking engine, no live chat, no instant scheduling link. For a high-ticket functional medicine clinic (1+ hour consultations, comprehensive panels, genetic testing), forcing patients through a phone/WhatsApp gate is the single largest leakage point.

**Severity:** Critical. High-intent patients researching functional medicine (as evidenced by Jeffrey Wright's review: *"From our personal research into Functional Medicine, we have been looking for a place..."*) are exactly the demographic least tolerant of manual booking friction.

---

### 🟠 Administrative Gaps — **CONFIRMED: MODERATE-TO-SEVERE**

**Evidence:**

The clinic's own 4-step process page states Step 01 is: *"We go deep – a detailed patient intake questionnaire, biometric measurements, and a review... of past patient medical records, and blood tests."*

This is a **detailed intake questionnaire** — yet there is **no evidence anywhere in the scrape of a digital intake link, patient portal, or pre-consultation form delivery mechanism**. The natural inference is that the "detailed intake questionnaire" is completed either (a) on paper at arrival, (b) via emailed PDF, or (c) verbally during the consultation. Any of these three options creates:

- **Duplicated data entry** (patient writes once, staff re-types into EMR)
- **Wasted consultation time** (the 1+ hour slot is partly consumed by intake)
- **Intake abandonment risk** (patients who booked but never received/returned the form show up incomplete → rushed or rescheduled consults)

The absence of any "Book → instant intake email" language in the patient-facing copy is a structural red flag for pre-consultation admin waste.

**Severity:** High operational cost. Every hour of doctor time consumed by intake administration instead of diagnosis is direct margin loss at functional medicine price points.

---

### 🟠 Communication Failures — **CONFIRMED: STRUCTURAL RISK**

**Evidence:**

1. **Sunday closure with zero automation signal.** Opening hours show *"Sunday: CLOSED"* and weekdays only 9:00AM–1:00PM (Monday) / 9:00AM–5:00PM (Tue–Sat). There is no visible after-hours auto-responder, no "we'll reply within X hours" SLA, no booking-so-you-don't-wait mechanism. Leads arriving via WhatsApp/phone on Sunday or after 5PM **have no autonomous path to booking** — they simply wait, and many won't.

2. **No post-consultation retention loop visible.** The 4-step process (Data Gathering → Consultation → Treatment → Evaluation & Review) is described **only as a clinical pathway, not as a communication cadence**. There is no mention of automated review reminders, treatment-plan follow-up nudges, or re-engagement triggers between Step 03 and Step 04. Reviews confirm patients *intend* to return (*"See you again at my next appointment!"* — sarahbasir) but nothing in the frontend architecture structurally ensures it.

3. **Review recency is a strength, but it masks a follow-up gap.** Nine Google reviews from April–May 2025 show genuine satisfaction — but satisfaction reviews are lagging indicators. The absence of any visible re-booking funnel (no "Book your follow-up" CTA, no patient portal login) means the loyalty being generated is **not being systematically harvested**.

**Severity:** Moderate-to-high. The clinic is clearly *earning* repeat intent — it appears to lack the *system* to capture it.
