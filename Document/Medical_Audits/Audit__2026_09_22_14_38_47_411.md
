## 1. Key Findings

### **Booking Friction: 🔴 CRITICAL SEVERITY (Confirmed)**

The evidence from the scrape reveals a **hard-blocked digital booking funnel**:

- **Cloudflare Turnstile Failure Loop:** The "Make an appointment" section contains a live, embedded Turnstile CAPTCHA that is actively displaying failure states in the scraped DOM — including "Checking your Browser…", "Verifying...", "Verification failed", "Verification expired" (×2), and "Stuck? Troubleshoot" prompts. This is **not a theoretical risk** — it is a *rendering artifact of a broken or stalled verification widget*. Any prospective patient hitting this wall on mobile (where Turnstile is notoriously flaky on slower Malaysian networks) will bounce. This is a textbook "bot-protection tax" that punishes real humans.
- **No Real-Time Calendar / Slot Picker:** The booking module only offers a `Select Doctor` dropdown (Dr Iman Thalia, Dr Iman Lim, Dr Krishnaveni Kanason, Dr Jevan Gunasenalan, Professor Rahmattulah Khan, Shelagh MacFarlane, Dr Lim Kheng Yee). There is **no visible date/time grid, no availability API, no instant confirmation**. The patient must submit a request and wait — meaning every booking is a *lead awaiting manual triage*, not a confirmed appointment.
- **Manual Channel Dependence:** Contact channels are limited to **Tel: +60 3 4810 6037** and **WhatsApp: +60 13 575 8121**. There is no online self-serve scheduling. Patients are implicitly funneled into phone calls or manual WhatsApp chats — both of which require staff to be at a desk to convert.
- **Trust Signal Mismatch:** The clinic markets itself as "sleek, modern and professional" (per patient Jeffrey Wright's review) — yet the booking UX is a CAPTCHA wall. The gap between brand promise and conversion reality is a leakage driver.

**Verdict:** Booking friction is severe and *actively blocking* conversions at the final step.

---

### **Administrative Gaps: 🟠 HIGH SEVERITY (Confirmed)**

The clinic's own 4-step patient management process exposes pre-consultation friction:

- **Step 01 — "Data Gathering":** The site explicitly states the process begins with *"a detailed patient intake questionnaire, biometric measurements, and a review (if applicable) of past patient medical records, and blood tests."* There is **no mention of a digital intake link sent post-booking**. The implied workflow is that this intake happens **in-clinic, on arrival** — consuming consultation-room time and front-desk capacity.
- **No Pre-Visit Digital Intake Pipeline:** Despite being a **functional medicine clinic** (a field that inherently requires deep history: gut health, biomarkers, genetics, glucose response, per the Wrights' review), there is **zero evidence of an emailed intake form, secure patient portal, or automated pre-consultation questionnaire dispatch**. Every new patient is a manual data-entry event.
- **Duplicated Entry Risk:** With no visible integration between the booking dropdown and any intake system, the same patient details (name, symptoms, history, doctor preference) are almost certainly **captured once at booking, then re-captured on paper or verbally at the clinic**.
- **"1+ Hour Consultation" Time Tax:** The site advertises a *"1+ hour consultation."* If any portion of that hour is spent on administrative intake rather than clinical diagnosis, the clinic is burning a high-value doctor's time on low-value data entry — directly capping daily patient throughput.

**Verdict:** Administrative gaps are high-severity. The clinic is trading billable doctor capacity for manual paperwork.

---

### **Communication Failures: 🟠 HIGH SEVERITY (Confirmed)**

Multiple structural weaknesses point to weak retention and after-hours leakage:

- **Weekend/After-Hours Void:** Opening hours are **Monday 9:00AM–1:00PM only**, **Tuesday–Saturday 9:00AM–5:00PM**, and **Sunday CLOSED**. This means **Sunday, Monday afternoons, and all evenings** are dead zones. A prospective patient researching functional medicine at 9PM on a Sunday (a peak research window) hits a Turnstile challenge, a phone number that won't answer, and a WhatsApp that won't reply until the next business day — by which time intent has cooled.
- **No Visible Automated Reminder System:** There is no mention of SMS/WhatsApp appointment reminders, confirmation sequences, or calendar integrations anywhere in the scrape. In a market where no-shows routinely run 15–25%, this is a silent revenue leak.
- **No Post-Consultation Retention Loop:** The site's Step 04 — *"Evaluation & Review: We monitor, review, and optimise"* — describes clinical review, but there is **no operational mechanism for structured post-visit follow-up**: no automated check-in at day 3/7/30, no re-engagement trigger for lapsed patients, no review-request automation, no recall campaign for the chronic-disease cohort (obesity, diabetes, hypertension) that inherently needs recurring touchpoints.
- **Review Sentiment is Positive but Reactive:** Reviews from Amelia Chen, Fatin Zaki, sarahbasir, Vinishyaa Sugumar, and others are glowing — but they are **organic Google reviews**, not the output of a structured NPS or retention loop. The clinic is *lucky* to have happy patients; it is not *systematically* capturing them.
- **Lead Drop at Booking Request:** Because the booking is a *request* not a *confirmation*, any lead that submits while the clinic is closed (or hits the Turnstile failure) is effectively **dropped with no automated acknowledgment or nurture sequence**.

**Verdict:** Communication failures are high-severity, particularly across after-hours windows and post-consultation retention.

### What We Propose to Build
DigitalRoot engineers **custom n8n workflow automations** tailored to The Rosewood Clinic's specific bottlenecks — not generic SaaS plug-ins.

1. **Frictionless Frontend Booking Calendar**
   - Replace the fragile Turnstile-gated request form with a **native, real-time availability calendar** (Google Calendar / Cal.com / Calendly integration).

2. **Automated WhatsApp / SMS Intake Pipeline**
   - The moment a booking is confirmed, an **n8n workflow fires** an instant WhatsApp/SMS with a secure intake link.
   - Patient completes the detailed functional medicine questionnaire **before arriving** — gut health, biomarkers, genetic history, lifestyle.
   - Responses populate directly into your EMR/Google Sheets/Notion, eliminating duplicated entry and freeing the "1+ hour consultation" entirely for clinical work.

3. **Multi-Channel Reminder & Confirmation Sequences**
   - Automated reminders at **T-48h, T-24h, and T-2h** via the patient's preferred channel (WhatsApp preferred in KL market).

   - Post-visit **review request automation** at T+24h to systematically harvest the 5-star reviews you currently get by luck.

4. **Structured Post-Consultation Retention Loops**
   - Automated check-ins at **Day 3, Day 7, Day 30** for treatment-plan adherence.
   - **Recall campaigns** for chronic-disease cohorts (diabetes, hypertension, obesity) at clinically appropriate intervals.
   - **Lapsed patient re-engagement** at 90/180 days via WhatsApp — protecting lifetime patient value.

5. **After-Hours Lead Capture & Nurture**
   - An **AI-powered WhatsApp responder** that engages Sunday/after-hours enquiries, captures contact details, offers calendar slots, and hands off to staff at 9AM.
   - Zero leads lost to the weekend void.
