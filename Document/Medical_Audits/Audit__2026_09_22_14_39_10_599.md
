# Operational Bottleneck Analysis: The Rosewood Clinic

## 1. Key Findings

### Booking Friction: **CONFIRMED — SEVERE**

The booking flow is critically compromised by a **broken Cloudflare Turnstile verification** embedded directly in the appointment scheduling module. The scraped text exposes a live failure loop captured mid-session:

> *"Checking your Browser… Verifying… Stuck? Troubleshoot… Verification failed… Verification expired… Refresh… Verification expired… Refresh…"*

This is not a hypothetical risk — this is the **actual rendered state of the booking widget** at the time of scrape. For a functional medicine clinic where a single patient lifetime value can run into thousands of ringgit (genetic testing, metabolic panels, multi-session treatment plans), every failed Turnstile verification is a **silent conversion kill**. The patient sees "Verification failed" and assumes the clinic is broken, unavailable, or untrustworthy — and leaves. There is no fallback path, no "book via WhatsApp instead" prompt, and no real-time calendar visibility. The only alternative channels are a static phone number (`+60 3 4810 6037`) and a WhatsApp line (`+60 13 575 8121`) — both requiring manual human response during operating hours. The clinic is also **closed on Sundays**, meaning any weekend booking intent is dropped entirely with zero automated capture.

Additionally, the doctor selection dropdown lists **six practitioners** (Dr Iman Thalia, Dr Iman Lim, Dr Krishnaveni Kanason, Dr Jevan Gunasenalan, Professor Rahmattulah Khan, Shelagh MacFarlane, Dr Lim Kheng Yee) with no indication of real-time availability, specialties, or next-open slots. The patient is forced to guess — a classic friction pattern that drives abandonment.

### Administrative Gaps: **CONFIRMED — SEVERE**

The clinic's own 4-step patient management process explicitly documents a **manual, pre-visit dependency chain**:

> *Step 01 — Data Gathering: "a detailed patient intake questionnaire, biometric measurements, and a review (if applicable) of past patient medical records, and blood tests."*

There is **no evidence of a digital intake link being sent post-booking**. The intake questionnaire is described as something that happens as part of the process — but the scrape shows no automated delivery mechanism (no "you'll receive an intake form via email/WhatsApp within X minutes" language). This means one of two failure modes is occurring:

1. **Patients arrive physically without completing intake**, forcing front-desk staff to hand them paper forms or walk them through questions on arrival — consuming consultation time and delaying the doctor.
2. **Intake is emailed as a PDF** that patients must print, fill, scan, and return — a workflow that generates drop-off, incomplete data, and rework.

Given that Step 02 is a **"1+ hour consultation,"** any administrative delay before the doctor enters the room directly cannibalizes billable clinical time. With six doctors on roster, even 15 minutes of avoided intake friction per patient per day compounds into hundreds of lost clinical hours annually.

### Communication Failures: **CONFIRMED — MODERATE-TO-SEVERE**

Three distinct signals point to a structured retention gap:

**Signal 1 — No automated reminder infrastructure visible.** The entire booking/contact surface contains zero mention of SMS, WhatsApp, or email reminders. For a clinic with a 1+ hour consultation model, no-shows are disproportionately expensive (the slot cannot be backfilled).

**Signal 2 — Sunday closure with no after-hours capture.** Opening hours show *"Sunday: CLOSED."* There is no chatbot, no auto-responder, no "leave your details and we'll book you Monday" mechanism. Any lead arriving Saturday evening through Sunday is lost unless they proactively re-engage.

**Signal 3 — Positive reviews reveal a recurring "next appointment" pattern with no automated loop.** Multiple reviewers explicitly say things like:

> *"See you again at my next appointment!"* (sarahbasir)
> *"Looking forward to working with her again."* (sarahbasir)
> *"We monitor, review, and optimise to achieve your health goals."* (Step 04)

Yet there is **no structured post-consultation retention trigger** — no automated review-request sequence, no re-engagement cadence, no "your follow-up is due" nudge. The clinic relies entirely on patient initiative and staff memory. This is a classic functional medicine failure mode: patients feel great after the first consult, then drift because no one systematically pulls them back for the review step (which is where the clinic's actual revenue and outcomes compound).
