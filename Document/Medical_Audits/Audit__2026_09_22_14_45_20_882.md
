## 1. Key Findings

### Booking Friction: **CONFIRMED — SEVERE**

The booking infrastructure exhibits critical conversion-path failures that directly leak high-intent patients.

**Evidence from scrape:**

- **Broken CAPTCHA/Turnstile verification.** The appointment booking widget shows repeated failure states embedded directly in the page markup: `Checking your Browser…` → `Verifying...` → `Success!` → `Verification failed` → `Verification expired` → `[Refresh]` → `Verification expired` → `[Refresh]`. This is not a hypothetical — the scrape captured **two consecutive "Verification expired" failure states** in a single render. A patient encountering a loop of expired verifications will abandon. This is the highest-severity leak in the funnel.
- **Doctor-dependent dropdown gate.** The booking form forces the patient to pre-select a specific practitioner from a list of **eight doctors** (`Dr Iman Thalia, Dr Iman Lim, Dr Krishnaveni Kanason, Dr Jevan Gunasenalan, Professor Dr Rahmattulah Khan, Shelagh MacFarlane, Dr Lim Kheng Yee`) *before* any calendar availability is shown. A new patient unfamiliar with the roster must either research each name or arbitrarily pick — friction that kills booking intent. There is **no "first available" or "best match / symptom-based triage" option visible**.
- **No real-time calendar/slot visibility.** The form presents doctor selection with no accompanying live availability. No self-serve slot picker is exposed. The entire conversion path is gated behind a challenge that is demonstrably failing.
- **Manual-reliant contact channels only.** The only fallback conversion paths are a fixed landline (`+60 3 4810 6037`) and a raw WhatsApp number (`+60 13 575 8121`) — both synchronous, staff-dependent, and only staffed during clinical hours. There is **no 24/7 digital booking**, no instant-confirmation flow, and no after-hours capture mechanism. A patient who discovers the clinic at 10pm Sunday (when the clinic is `CLOSED`) hits a broken Turnstile and two manual channels that will not respond — total leak.

**Diagnosis:** The clinic is investing heavily (strong Google reviews, paid doctor roster, video content) to drive *attention*, then converting it through a **fragile, verification-loop-prone, doctor-gated form with no live calendar and no non-human fallback**. This is textbook high-intent patient leakage.

---

### Administrative Gaps: **CONFIRMED — SEVERE**

The clinic's own published 4-step process explicitly reveals a **manual-first, arrival-dependent intake model** with no digital pre-consultation pipeline.

**Evidence from scrape:**

- **Step 01 "Data Gathering" is described as:** *"a detailed patient intake questionnaire, biometric measurements, and a review (if applicable) of past patient medical records, and blood tests."* The description implies the **intake questionnaire is a distinct, heavy step conducted as part of the visit process** — with no evidence anywhere in the scrape of a **digital intake link sent automatically at the moment of booking**. There is no patient portal, no pre-visit form URL, no "complete your intake online before your visit" CTA. The default assumption is paper/clinic-side completion.
- **Step 02 "Consultation & Tests" is a 1+ hour appointment:** *"The 1+ hour consultation with our doctor..."* — For a functional medicine clinic, the intake questionnaire is typically long (symptom history, lifestyle, diet, biomarkers, timelines). If even **20–30 minutes** of that 1-hour slot is spent on data capture that could have been collected before arrival, the clinic is burning **expensive doctor time on data entry** — a direct capacity and margin leak.
- **No duplicated-entry controls visible.** The booking flow (doctor dropdown + Turnstile) is completely decoupled from the intake step. Whatever data the patient enters at booking is **not** shown to flow into the 4-step intake process — implying re-entry. Duplicated data entry = error surface + friction + staff time.
- **Records review is "if applicable."** *"a review (if applicable) of past patient medical records, and blood tests"* — the conditional framing suggests this is handled ad hoc, manually, per-patient, with no structured pre-consultation document-request pipeline. Every patient who shows up without prior records forces the clinic to either delay, reschedule testing, or proceed with incomplete data.

**Diagnosis:** The clinic's flagship differentiator — a "deep," data-rich, 4-step functional medicine process — is undermined by a **manual, arrival-dependent intake model** that (a) wastes billable doctor time, (b) creates no structured data pipeline, and (c) leaves record-request proactively to chance.

### Communication Failures: **CONFIRMED — SEVERE**

The operational structure reveals **zero structured retention loops, no visible automated reminders, and a hard Sunday/after-hours dead zone** that drops leads.

**Evidence from scrape:**

- **Hard closed window with no capture.** Opening hours: `Monday: 9:00AM – 1:00PM`, `Tuesday – Saturday: 9:00AM – 5:00PM`, `Sunday: CLOSED`. Note the **Monday half-day (9am–1pm only)** and **full Sunday closure**. Any lead arriving Sunday, or Monday afternoon, hits a `CLOSED` clinic with no automated responder. Combined with the broken Turnstile, the lead simply evaporates.
- **No automated reminder or follow-up evidence.** Nowhere in the scrape is there any mention of booking confirmations, SMS/WhatsApp reminders, waitlist automation, or re-engagement campaigns. The only post-visit signal is **Google reviews** — which are glowing but *organic and unmanaged*.
- **Retention loop is manual and review-dependent.** The testimonials (`Amelia Chen`, `sarahbasir`, `Vinishyaa Sugumar`, etc.) all praise *human warmth and staff friendliness* — but multiple reviewers explicitly reference **"my next appointment"** and **"looking forward to working with her again"**, i.e., the retention/continuity motion is **person-dependent, not system-dependent**. If a patient's preferred doctor is fully booked or the front desk is overwhelmed, the follow-up simply doesn't happen. There is no evidence of an automated "time for your 3-month review" trigger — despite the clinic's own **Step 04 "Evaluation & Review"** explicitly promising *"We monitor, review, and optimise to achieve your health goals."* That promise is being fulfilled manually, if at all.
- **No lead-nurture for the long consideration cycle.** Functional medicine is high-consideration and high-ticket (comprehensive panels, genetic testing, metabolic assessments per reviewer `Jeffrey Wright`). Leads who aren't ready to book on first visit need nurture. There is **no visible email/WhatsApp drip, no education sequence, no "not ready yet?" capture** — so cold and warm leads alike fall through.
- **WhatsApp is a raw number, not a managed channel.** `WhatsApp: +60 13 575 8121` with no auto-reply, no business-hours routing, no FAQ bot, no booking handoff. Every message must be manually seen and answered by staff — creating response-time variance that directly correlates with lost bookings.

**Diagnosis:** The clinic has *excellent* human service (reviews prove it) but **no operational communication scaffolding**. Reminders, intake dispatch, after-hours response, review-generation, and the promised Step-04 evaluation loop are all manual, inconsistent, and dependent on staff availability during shrinking clinical hours.

### The Situation

The Rosewood Clinic has built something rare: a genuinely loved functional medicine practice with a 4.9-star-class review profile, a deep clinical methodology, and strong demand signals. Your problem is **not** reputation or demand. Your problem is that your **booking funnel is breaking under a Cloudflare Turnstile loop**, your **intake process still depends on arrival-day data capture**, and your **retention and after-hours communication rely entirely on manual staff effort** during limited clinical hours (with Sunday fully closed and Monday limited to a half-day).

In Lean Six Sigma terms: you have high-quality demand entering a process with three failure modes — a **conversion gate that fails (Turnstile)**, a **non-value-add data step (manual intake)**, and **unstaffed queue time (nights/weekends/Sundays)**. Every one of these leaks patients you already paid to attract.

### What We Propose to Build

We will engineer a set of **custom n8n workflow automations** that treat the root operational cause — not patch the symptoms — across all three failure modes:

**1. A Frictionless Frontend Booking Calendar (Kills the Turnstile Leak)**
- Replace the fragile, verification-looping doctor-dropdown form with a **custom, real-time availability calendar** pulling live slots from your practitioner schedules.
- Add a **"First Available / Best Match"** path and symptom-based triage so new patients are never forced to research eight doctors before booking.
- Embed the calendar on-site with graceful fallback: if any challenge fails, the lead is **captured and queued**, never lost.

**2. An Automated Intake Pipeline (Kills the Manual-Data Leak)**
- The instant a booking is confirmed, n8n fires a **personalized intake link** (WhatsApp + email) with your digital questionnaire, document-upload for past records/blood tests, and pre-visit instructions.
- Data flows structured into your records system — **eliminating duplicate entry** and returning 20–30 minutes of every 1-hour consultation to actual clinical care.
- Auto-reminders and records-request chase-sequences run silently until completion.

**3. A Structured Retention & After-Hours Communication Loop (Kills the Retention/Lead-Drop Leak)**
- **Automated WhatsApp/SMS reminders** at booking, 48h, and 2h pre-visit (slashes no-shows).
- **After-hours auto-responder + lead capture** on WhatsApp and web — every Sunday and Monday-afternoon lead is engaged, qualified, and booked into the next open slot automatically.
- **Step-04 retention triggers:** automated "time for your 3-month review" and "how are your health goals going?" sequences via WhatsApp/email — making your promised Evaluation & Review step systematic instead of aspirational.
- **Automated review-request flow** post-visit to compound your already-strong social proof, plus a **dormant-patient re-engagement campaign**.
