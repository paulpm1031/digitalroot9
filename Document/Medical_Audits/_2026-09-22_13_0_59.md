# Operational Bottleneck Analysis: The Rosewood Clinic

## Diagnosed Process Challenges

After analyzing The Rosewood Clinic's website, booking flow, and 8 patient reviews, I've identified the following operational bottlenecks:

### 1. 🚫 Booking Friction — **CRITICAL**
The appointment booking flow is gated behind a **Cloudflare Turnstile CAPTCHA ("Checking your Browser… Verifying...")**. This is the single largest friction point. The snippet shows repeated failure states: *"Verification failed," "Verification expired," "Stuck? Troubleshoot."* A prospective patient — often someone unwell, fatigued, or in pain — hitting a CAPTCHA loop will abandon the booking and never return. There is no visible alternative digital booking path (no Calendly, no instant WhatsApp deep-link, no self-serve calendar).

### 2. 📋 Administrative Gaps — **HIGH**
The clinic's own 4-step process (Step 01: "Data Gathering" — detailed intake questionnaire, biometrics, past medical records) implies a **lengthy manual intake process**. There is **no visible digital patient intake form** on the site. For a functional medicine clinic running 1+ hour consults and comprehensive blood panels, manual/paper intake is a massive time sink and a drop-off point. Patients fill out forms twice, arrive unprepared, and delay appointment start times.

### 3. ⚠️ Communication Failures — **MODERATE / HIDDEN**
Reviews are glowing *after* the consultation — but notice the pattern: every review praises the **human touch** (Dr. Deanna, Dr. Krish, Husna the staff). This suggests the clinic's backend is likely **manual and person-dependent**. There's no evidence of automated appointment reminders, post-consultation follow-up sequences, or recall nudges for the "Evaluation & Review" (Step 04) stage. In a cash-pay functional medicine model, **missed follow-ups = lost lifetime value and churn.**
