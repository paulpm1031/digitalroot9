# Operational Bottleneck Analysis: The Rosewood Clinic

## Key Findings

After analyzing the website content and patient testimonials, I've identified **critical operational gaps** that are likely driving potential patients away:

---

### 🚨 Bottleneck #1: Severe Booking Friction

**Evidence:**
- The booking section shows a **Cloudflare Turnstile CAPTCHA** that repeatedly fails ("Verification failed," "Verification expired," "Checking your Browser… Verifying...")
- No visible calendar/scheduling widget — patients must select from a dropdown and hope for the best
- Phone (+60 3 4810 6037) and WhatsApp are listed as primary contact methods, implying manual coordination is required
- **7 doctor options** in a dropdown with no availability visibility — creates decision paralysis and requires back-and-forth

**Impact:** Patients who are motivated to book hit a wall of bot-verification loops and uncertainty. High-intent visitors abandon within seconds.

---

### 🚨 Bottleneck #2: Administrative Gaps in Patient Intake

**Evidence:**
- The 4-step process explicitly mentions *"a detailed patient intake questionnaire"* as Step 1
- No digital intake form is linked anywhere on the page
- No mention of pre-consultation portals, document upload, or medical history digitization
- Reviews praise the *"1+ hour consultation"* — meaning valuable doctor time is likely spent on data gathering that could be automated

**Impact:** New patients likely fill paper forms or PDFs, then repeat information verbally. This wastes clinician time, delays treatment plans, and creates a clunky first impression for a premium-priced functional medicine clinic.

---

### 🚨 Bottleneck #3: Communication & Follow-Up Blind Spots

**Evidence:**
- Multiple reviews mention *"Looking forward to working with her again"* and *"See you again at my next appointment"* — but no automated rebooking or reminder system is evident
- Step 4 ("Evaluation & Review") claims monitoring and optimization, but there's no visible patient portal, check-in cadence, or automated follow-up
- No mention of post-consultation summaries, test result notifications, or treatment adherence nudges
- International patients (like Jeffrey Wright, who was *"traveling for a year"*) would struggle with timezone-dependent phone follow-ups

**Impact:** Patients fall through the cracks between visits. For a clinic charging premium rates for comprehensive care, silent gaps erode trust and reduce lifetime value.
