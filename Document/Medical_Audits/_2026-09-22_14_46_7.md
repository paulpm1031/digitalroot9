# Operational Bottleneck Analysis: The Rosewood Clinic

## Process Challenges Identified

### 1. Booking Friction ⚠️ **CRITICAL**
The appointment booking flow reveals a **severe friction point**:
- A "Make an appointment" section exists with a doctor dropdown menu, but the form is gated behind a **Cloudflare Turnstile CAPTCHA** that repeatedly shows:
  - "Checking your Browser…"
  - "Verifying..."
  - "Verification failed"
  - "Verification expired" (appearing **twice**)
  - Multiple "Troubleshoot" and "Refresh" prompts
- Patients who experience CAPTCHA failures are forced to abandon digital booking and **resort to phone (+60 3 4810 6037) or WhatsApp (+60 13 575 8121)** — both of which require manual coordination during limited business hours.
- No visible calendar-based self-scheduling, no instant confirmation, no after-hours booking capability.

**Impact:** Every failed CAPTCHA is a lost patient. High-intent users (like Jeffrey Wright, who was "traveling for a year" looking for a clinic) may bounce to competitors.

### 2. Administrative Gaps ⚠️ **MODERATE**
The website describes a **4-step patient management process**:
1. Data Gathering (detailed patient intake questionnaire, biometrics, medical records review)
2. Consultation & Tests (1+ hour consultation)
3. Treatment
4. Evaluation & Review

**However, no digital intake mechanism is visible on the site.** There is no:
- Online patient intake form
- Secure document upload portal for prior medical records/blood tests
- Digital consent or health history link

This suggests patients likely complete paper forms or email documents — creating bottlenecks at Step 1 and delaying Step 2 (the 1-hour consult). This is especially problematic for a functional medicine clinic that prides itself on *deep* data gathering.

### 3. Communication Failures ✅ **LOW (but risk exists)**
Reviews are overwhelmingly positive on staff friendliness and follow-up tone ("See you again at my next appointment!"). **No explicit follow-up complaints appear.** However, the lack of any visible:
- Automated appointment reminders
- Post-consultation follow-up sequences
- Review-request automation (despite strong reviews, there's no apparent system)

...represents a **latent risk**. As patient volume scales, manual follow-up will break.
