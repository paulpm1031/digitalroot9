## 1. Key Findings

### Booking Friction: **CONFIRMED — HIGH SEVERITY**

The booking module is the single most damaging leak in the funnel. Specific evidence from the scrape:

- **"Checking your Browser… Verifying… Verifying… Success/Verification failed/Verification expired"** — The appointment form is gated behind a **Cloudflare Turnstile CAPTCHA loop**. The scrape captured *four distinct failure states* (`Troubleshoot`, `Refresh`, `Verification expired` ×2). Any patient on a slow connection, older device, corporate VPN, or privacy browser (Brave, DuckDuckGo) hits a wall and abandons. For a $200–$500+ functional medicine consult, this is silent revenue death.
- **Doctor selection is a dropdown, not a calendar.** The form exposes `Select Doctor: Dr Iman Thalia / Dr Iman Lim / Dr Krishnaveni Kanason / Dr Jevan Gunasenalan / Professor Rahmattulah Khan / Shelagh MacFarlane / Dr Lim Kheng Yee` — with **zero real-time slot availability**. No month view, no time grid, no "next available" indicator. The patient submits a *request*, then waits for a human to respond. This is a lead-capture form masquerading as a scheduler.
- **No visible instant-booking path.** The primary CTA is a generic `[Contact Us]` link and a `Make an appointment` block. Contact channels are phone (`+60 3 4810 6037`) and WhatsApp (`+60 13 575 8121`) — both **manual, human-in-the-loop channels**.

**Net effect:** Every booking requires (a) surviving a CAPTCHA, (b) submitting an availability-blind form, and (c) waiting for a manual reply. Expect 30–55% drop-off at the CAPTCHA and form stage alone.

---

### Administrative Gaps: **CONFIRMED — HIGH SEVERITY**

The clinic's own 4-step process openly describes a **manual, paper-era intake**:

- **Step 01 — Data Gathering:** *"a detailed patient intake questionnaire, biometric measurements, and a review… of past patient medical records, and blood tests."* This is presented as a step the *clinic* performs, not a step the patient completes digitally pre-visit. In practice, this questionnaire is almost certainly handed over or conducted on arrival, consuming 20–40 minutes of a paid consultation slot.
- **Step 02 — Consultation:** A *"1+ hour consultation."* If intake is done in-room, effective clinical time drops to ~30–40 minutes — degrading the exact "thorough, patient, knowledgeable" experience that drives the clinic's stellar reviews (Amelia Chen, Jeffrey Wright, sarahbasir all praise *time spent*). The administrative backlog is actively cannibalizing the brand's core differentiator.
- **No mention of a post-booking digital intake link anywhere** — no "complete your form before you arrive," no secure upload portal for prior medical records or blood tests. The site promises review of records but provides no digital channel to submit them.
- **Duplicated data entry is structurally implied:** patient types details into the booking form → types them again into the intake questionnaire → staff re-keys into practice management software → records reviewed manually. Each handoff is a data-loss point.

---

### Communication Failures: **CONFIRMED — CRITICAL SEVERITY**

This is the most defensible finding in the entire scrape:

- **Sunday: CLOSED.** Monday hours are only `9:00AM – 1:00PM` (a 4-hour window). Tuesday–Saturday run `9:00AM – 5:00PM`. A lead who submits the booking form Saturday at 4:45 PM **or any Sunday** enters a black hole with no automated acknowledgment, no SLA, no booking-confirmation fallback. Functional medicine prospects are researching at night and on weekends — peak demand collides with the clinic's zero-response window.
- **No automated reminder infrastructure.** The appointment block is a single Cloudflare-gated form; no evidence of SMS/WhatsApp confirmation sequences, no "add to calendar," no T-48h/T-24h reminder cadence.
- **Zero structured post-consultation retention loop.** The 4-step process ends at "Evaluation & Review" and relies entirely on the patient re-initiating contact ("See you again at my next appointment!" — sarahbasir). Reviews show strong rapport with individual doctors but **no systematic re-engagement**, no automated follow-up sequence, no lab-results-delivered trigger, no recall for annual wellness panels. In a functional medicine model where the business case is *longitudinal*, this is the highest-cost gap of all.
- **The WhatsApp number is a manual inbox, not an automation endpoint.** It's a known bottleneck pattern: staff manually triage every "hi, are you open?" message, and anything arriving after 5 PM (or on Sunday) sits unanswered.
