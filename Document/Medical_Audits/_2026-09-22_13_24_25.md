More damning: the booking flow is gated behind a **Cloudflare Turnstile CAPTCHA** that visibly fails on-page — the text itself shows "Verification failed," "Verification expired," and "Stuck? Troubleshoot" states. A prospective patient who has already decided to book is now fighting a bot-check widget. High-intent patients researching at 9pm on a Sunday hit a wall.

### 2. Administrative Gaps — **SEVERE**

Step 01 of their own 4-step process is "Data Gathering: a detailed patient intake questionnaire, biometric measurements, and review of past medical records, and blood tests." There is **zero digital intake mechanism anywhere on the page** — no form link, no patient portal, no upload for past blood tests or medical records. This means the "detailed intake questionnaire" and records review almost certainly happen as paper forms in-clinic or via email/WhatsApp back-and-forth. For a clinic selling **genetic testing, metabolic panels, and comprehensive bloodwork**, asking patients to hand-carry or email records is a conversion killer — and it eats into the expensive 1-hour consultation slot doing data entry instead of doctoring.

### 3. Communication Failures — **LATENT / STRUCTURAL**

Reviews are glowing on *in-person* experience (Dr. Deanna, Dr. Krish, Dr. Iman all named positively), but notice what's absent: **no review mentions follow-up, results delivery, or between-visit communication**.
