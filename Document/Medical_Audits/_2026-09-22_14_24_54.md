# Operational Bottleneck Analysis: The Rosewood Clinic

## 1. Key Findings

### **Booking Friction:** ✅ **CONFIRMED — HIGH SEVERITY**

**Evidence from scrape:**

The booking widget reveals a **critical digital conversion failure**. The "Make an appointment" section displays a Cloudflare Turnstile CAPTCHA that is actively failing:

> *"Checking your Browser… Verifying... Stuck? Troubleshoot. Verification failed. Verification expired. Refresh."*

This is a **live, unresolved booking barrier**. Every patient attempting to book online is hitting a broken bot-verification wall. The presence of "Troubleshoot" and multiple "Refresh" prompts indicates this is a recurring, systemic issue — not a one-time glitch.

**Additional friction indicators:**

- The only visible contact paths are a **phone number (+60 3 4810 6037)** and a **WhatsApp number (+60 13 575 8121)** — both requiring manual, human-initiated contact during business hours.
- **No real-time digital calendar** or instant slot-selection is visible. The form only shows a dropdown to "Select Doctor" — implying a request-based, not confirmation-based, booking flow.
- For a clinic treating chronic conditions and offering premium wellness panels (metabolic assessments, genetic testing), the inability to book online represents a **direct revenue leak**. Patients researching at 11 PM on a Sunday (when the clinic is closed) cannot convert.

**Verdict:** The broken Turnstile + manual-only contact = **high-friction conversion path actively driving patient leakage.**

---

### **Administrative Gaps:** ✅ **CONFIRMED — MODERATE-HIGH SEVERITY**

**Evidence from scrape:**

The clinic's own 4-step patient management process explicitly states Step 01 is:

> *"We go deep – a detailed patient intake questionnaire, biometric measurements, and a review (if applicable) of past patient medical records, and blood tests."*

There is **no mention of a digital intake link being sent post-booking**. The process describes what happens but not *how* or *when* the intake questionnaire reaches the patient. The absence of any "digital intake" or "online form" language strongly suggests this "detailed patient intake questionnaire" is completed **in-clinic upon arrival** or via paper/PDF.

**Operational impact:**

- Patients arrive early to fill out lengthy forms, consuming staff time and delaying consultations.
- Doctors receive intake data **at the point of consultation** rather than in advance, reducing preparation quality.
- For a 1+ hour consultation model, every minute spent on manual intake is a minute not spent on billable care.
- No proactive digital intake link = **duplicated data entry** (patient writes, staff reads/transcribes, doctor re-reads).

**Verdict:** The "detailed intake questionnaire" is a manual, in-person bottleneck with zero evidence of automated digital pre-consultation intake.

---

### **Communication Failures:** ✅ **CONFIRMED — HIGH SEVERITY**

**Evidence from scrape:**

- **Opening Hours:** Monday 9AM–1PM (half day), Tuesday–Saturday 9AM–5PM, **Sunday: CLOSED**.
- **WhatsApp is listed as a contact method** — but there is no evidence of automated WhatsApp responses, booking confirmations, or reminders. WhatsApp is a manual channel requiring staff availability.
- **No automated appointment reminders** are mentioned anywhere in the scrape.
- **No structured post-consultation retention loop** is visible. The 4-step process ends at "Evaluation & Review" but there is no automation layer — no follow-up sequences, no re-engagement triggers, no recall reminders for repeat bloodwork or annual assessments.
- **After-hours gap:** Sunday closure + Monday half-day = **leads arriving during closed windows sit unattended** until staff return. In functional medicine, where patients are often researching and ready to commit after hours, this is a significant leakage point.

**Patient review evidence (indirect):**

Reviews are overwhelmingly positive about *in-person* care ("thorough," "patient," "kind," "friendly staff"). But the reviews are all from **April–May 2025** and cluster around the same doctors. There is **no review mentioning ease of booking, digital convenience, or follow-up communication** — suggesting the digital experience is not a memorable positive, or worse, not experienced at all.

**Verdict:** Manual WhatsApp + no automated reminders + after-hours closure + no retention loops = **systemic communication failure driving patient drop-off.**
