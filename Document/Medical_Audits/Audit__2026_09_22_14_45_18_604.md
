## 1. Key Findings

### **Booking Friction: CONFIRMED — SEVERE**

The scrape reveals a **booking flow that actively sabotages conversion at the final step.**

- **Evidence:** The "Make an appointment" section renders a doctor selector dropdown followed immediately by a **Cloudflare Turnstile CAPTCHA widget** displaying: *"Checking your Browser… Verifying… Verification failed / Verification expired — Refresh."*
- **Impact:** This is a **hard-fail point.** Patients with privacy-hardened browsers, VPNs (common among the clinic's expat/international demographic — see Jeffrey Wright's review: *"We've been traveling for a year"*), or stale sessions are bounced with a "Verification failed" wall. There is **no fallback** — no direct phone CTA beneath the widget, no WhatsApp shortcut, no graceful degradation. The patient is simply ejected.
- **Compounding friction:** There is **no real-time calendar/slot visibility** anywhere in the scrape. Patients select a doctor but cannot see availability, forcing a downstream manual scheduling step (phone tag or WhatsApp ping-pong).
- **Booking channels are manual and rigid:** Only a landline (`+60 3 4810 6037`) and a WhatsApp number (`+60 13 575 8121`) are offered. WhatsApp is *not* at stated as offering structured booking — meaning every enquiry is a human-mediated chat thread that dies when staff go offline.
- **Verdict:** For a clinic charging premium functional-medicine rates with a 1+ hour consultation model, a broken CAPTCHA-gated form is a **silent funnel hemorrhage.**

### **Administrative Gaps: CONFIRMED — SEVERE**

The clinic's own 4-step process publicly documents a **manual, pre-visit data burden** with no evidence of digital automation.

- **Evidence:** Step 01 "Data Gathering" explicitly states: *"a detailed patient intake questionnaire, biometric measurements, and a review of past patient medical records, and blood tests."* Nothing in the scrape indicates this questionnaire is delivered **digitally, instantly, post-booking.**
- **Impact:** Best-case, staff email a PDF — patient prints, hand-writes, and returns. Worst-case (common in practice), the intake is completed **on arrival**, collapsing golden-hour consultation time into clerical data entry. For a clinic whose core value proposition is a *"1+ hour consultation"* (Step 02), losing even 15 minutes to paperwork is a **25% capacity tax per patient.**
- **Duplication risk:** With no visible CRM, intake → consultation → treatment → review (Steps 01–04) implies the same data is re-keyed across multiple touchpoints. No integration layer is advertised.
- **Verdict:** This is a clinic running a **premium clinical model on startup-grade manual admin.** The bottleneck is invisible in Google reviews (patients praise the *doctors*, not the logistics) — which means it's tolerated, not fixed.

### **Communication Failures: CONFIRMED — SEVERE**

The operational structure shows **zero automated retention architecture**, and the review pattern quietly proves it.

- **Evidence (schedule):** Monday: 9AM–1PM only. Sunday: **CLOSED.** Tuesday–Saturday: 9AM–5PM. That's **roughly 45 hours of live-response coverage per week.** Any booking enquiry, reschedule request, or post-consult question arriving outside that window (i.e., the majority of working adults' free time) lands in a dead zone with no automated acknowledgement.
- **Evidence (no reminder/follow-up loop):** Multiple reviews read as **one-and-done testimonials** — *"Looking forward to working with her again"* (sarahbasir), *"See you again at my next appointment!"* — but there is **no structured retention mechanism visible**: no automated booking reminder, no post-consultation check-in sequence, no re-test/review trigger despite Step 04 explicitly being *"Evaluation & Review."*
- **Retention gap multiplied by clinical model:** Functional medicine is inherently **longitudinal** (gut protocols, hormone titration, glucose response tracking). A patient who completes one consult and receives no automated 2-week / 6-week / 12-week check-in is a **protocol that quietly stalls** — and a patient who never rebooks.
- **Verdict:** The clinic is winning the *first* impression (reviews are glowing) and **losing the second, third, and fourth appointments** to silence.

### The Diagnosis

Your doctors are exceptional — your reviews prove it. Your **operations are leaving money and patients on the table** at three specific points:

1. **A CAPTCHA wall is rejecting bookings** from privacy-conscious and international patients before they ever reach your calendar.
2. **Intake paperwork is consuming consultation time** that patients are paying premium rates for.
3. **A 45-hour live-response week and zero retention automation** means every after-hours enquiry and every discharged protocol is a silent churn event.

These are not marketing problems. They are **plumbing problems** — and they are fixable with engineering.

### The Prescription

DigitalRoot proposes a **three-workflow n8n automation build**, deployed to your existing stack, treating each root cause:

**Workflow 1 — Frictionless Booking Layer**
Replace the CAPTCHA-gated, non-functional form with a **self-hosted real-time booking calendar** (Cal.com/Calendly embedded or custom) with doctor/slot visibility, timezone handling, and — critically — a **zero-friction fallback path** (one-tap WhatsApp deep link + click-to-call) if any verification step fails. Bot protection moves server-side and silent.

**Workflow 2 — Instant Intake Pipeline**
The moment a booking confirms, n8n auto-fires: (a) a **secure digital intake form link** (typed, validated, HIPAA/PDPA-aware storage) to WhatsApp or email, (b) a 24-hour and 2-hour reminder if unsubmitted, and (c) a **pre-consultation data pack** delivered to the doctor's dashboard before the patient walks in. Manual re-keying eliminated.

**Workflow 3 — Retention & Response Engine**
- **After-hours autoresponder** on WhatsApp: instant acknowledgement + self-serve booking link, so 5PM–9AM and Sundays stop bleeding leads.
- **Automated appointment reminders** (T-48h, T-24h, T-2h) with one-tap reschedule.
- **Post-consultation retention loops:** triggered sequences at Day 3 (check-in), Day 14 (protocol adherence), and clinically appropriate review intervals for Step 04 — turning one-time testimonials into recurring patients.
