## Operational Bottleneck Analysis: The Rosewood Clinic

### Process Challenge #1: Booking Friction — SEVERE
- The only booking pathway offered is a **"Make an appointment" form gated behind a Cloudflare Turnstile CAPTCHA** — which frequently fails, expires, and forces users into a "Troubleshoot/Refresh" loop before they can even submit a request.
- No real-time calendar, no instant confirmation, no self-service rescheduling. Patients must submit a request and **wait for a manual callback** to confirm a slot.
- Doctor selection is a blind dropdown (8 practitioners, no availability visibility), and the form gives no indication of response time.
- **Impact:** High-intent patients (especially the traveling/executive demographic visible in reviews, e.g., the Wrights) abandon at the CAPTCHA or go to a competitor with instant online booking.

### Process Challenge #2: Administrative Gaps — MODERATE
- Step 01 of their own 4-step process is a **"detailed patient intake questionnaire"** — but there is no digital intake link, portal, or pre-visit form anywhere on the site.
- This means intake is almost certainly handled via paper forms or phone calls at/around the appointment, consuming staff time and delaying the 1+ hour consultation.
- No visible patient portal for records, test results, or follow-up documentation.

### Process Challenge #3: Communication Failures — LOW-TO-MODERATE
- Reviews are overwhelmingly positive (a strength), but they hint at a **post-consultation communication gap**: patients praise the *consultation* but mention "looking forward to working with her again" and multi-step plans with no visible automated follow-up, recall, or review-request system.
- With a 4-step process ending in "Evaluation & Review," there is no automated mechanism ensuring patients actually return for reviews — a revenue leak, not just a CX issue.
- The booking form's CAPTCHA failures create a silent communication breakdown: patients who fail verification may never know if their request went through.
