# Operational Bottleneck Analysis: The Rosewood Clinic

## Identified Process Challenges

### 1. Booking Friction — **CRITICAL**
The "Make an appointment" section reveals two compounding problems:

- **Doctor selection dropdown with 6+ practitioners** but *no visible availability, specialties, or booking calendar* — patients must guess who to see, then likely wait for a manual callback to confirm.
- **Cloudflare Turnstile CAPTCHA actively failing** on the booking widget ("Checking your Browser… Verifying… Verification failed / Verification expired"). This is a **live, revenue-killing bug**. Every patient who hits "Verification failed" and doesn't troubleshoot is a lost booking. The repeated "Refresh" prompts indicate a broken challenge loop — likely a misconfigured Turnstile key or domain mismatch.
- **No online scheduling integration** (no Calendly, Cal.com, or native calendar). Combined with the fact that **the clinic is closed Sundays and only open 9AM–1PM Mondays**, patients browsing outside these narrow windows cannot self-serve.

### 2. Administrative Gaps — **HIGH**
The 4-step patient management process explicitly starts with:
> *"a detailed patient intake questionnaire, biometric measurements, and a review (if applicable) of past patient medical records, and blood tests."*

This is described as a **manual, in-clinic data gathering step** preceding a **1+ hour consultation**. For a functional medicine clinic running comprehensive panels (biomarkers, gut health, genetic testing, glucose response — per Jeffrey Wright's review), paper or PDF intake forms create:
- Pre-visit friction (patients must arrive early or complete forms on-site)
- No structured data pipeline into the doctor's workflow
- Delays in the "Data Gathering → Consultation" handoff, directly eating into the 1-hour billable consult window

### 3. Communication Failures — **MODERATE / LATENT**
Reviews are glowing, but the *structure* of the funnel reveals risk:
- The **"Evaluation & Review" (Step 04)** — *"We monitor, review, and optimise"* — implies iterative follow-up, yet there is **no visible patient portal, reminder system, or automated follow-up mechanism** on the site.
- Patients like sarahbasir mention "See you again at my next appointment!" — recurring visits are the revenue model, but there's no automated re-engagement, test-result notification, or post-consult summary delivery described.
- **No WhatsApp booking automation** despite listing a WhatsApp number — it's a manual inbox, not a channel.

**Net effect:** A patient impressed by the reviews lands on a page, hits a broken CAPTCHA, can't see doctor availability, must call during limited hours, fills out intake manually, and relies on staff to remember their follow-up. Each step leaks conversion.
