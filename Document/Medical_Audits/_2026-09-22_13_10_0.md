# Operational Bottleneck Analysis: The Rosewood Clinic

## Identified Process Challenges

### 1. Booking Friction ⚠️ **CRITICAL**
**Evidence from text:**
- Appointment booking requires selecting from a dropdown of 7 doctors with no indicated availability
- Cloudflare Turnstile verification layer creates friction ("Verifying...", "Verification failed", "Verification expired", "Stuck? Troubleshoot")
- Only phone (+60 3 4810 6037) and WhatsApp (+60 13 575 8121) listed as contact methods — **no digital self-scheduling**
- No online calendar, no real-time availability, no instant confirmation
- Patients must initiate contact during business hours only (Mon 9-1, Tue-Sat 9-5, Sun closed)

**Operational impact:** Prospective patients researching clinics at 10pm on Sunday hit a wall. The friction between "I want to book" and "I have a confirmed appointment" spans multiple touchpoints and manual coordination.

---

### 2. Administrative Gaps ⚠️ **HIGH**
**Evidence from text:**
- Step 01 "Data Gathering" describes: "detailed patient intake questionnaire, biometric measurements, and review of past medical records, and blood tests" — with no mention of digital intake
- Step 02 involves a "1+ hour consultation" — suggesting intake is likely collected on-site, consuming clinical time
- No patient portal, no pre-visit digital forms, no document upload link mentioned
- Past medical records "review (if applicable)" — implies manual retrieval process
- The single-page contact form feeds directly into a Cloudflare challenge rather than a structured intake flow

**Operational impact:** The 1-hour consultation is likely padded with administrative data collection that could happen asynchronously before the visit.

---

### 3. Communication Failures ⚠️ **MODERATE**
**Evidence from text:**
- Step 04 "Evaluation & Review" — vague: "We monitor, review, and optimise" with no defined cadence, channel, or automation
- No mention of automated appointment reminders
- Reviews are uniformly positive, but notably **all 8 reviews are clustered April–May 2025** — suggesting either recent opening or inconsistent review solicitation
- Long patient testimonials (Jeffrey Wright) describe self-directed research to find the clinic — patients are arriving pre-educated, which means the clinic isn't capturing that demand earlier in the funnel
- No follow-up automation visible for the "Evaluation & Review" step despite it being core to the 4-step methodology

**Operational impact:** The clinic's entire differentiator is its 4-step process, yet steps 1 and 4 have no visible automation — meaning the "concierge" feel depends entirely on manual staff labor, which doesn't scale.

3. **Your Evaluation & Review step has no automated engine.** "We monitor, review, and optimise" is a promise that currently depends entirely on manual follow-through — the exact step where chronic-care patients churn.

**Fixes: Booking Friction**

### Workflow 2: Digital Intake & Records Pipeline
**Fixes: Administrative Gaps**

- **Smart intake link** sent immediately upon booking — patients complete the "detailed patient intake questionnaire" from home, on mobile, before their visit
- **Document upload portal** for past medical records and blood test results — no more "review (if applicable)" ambiguity; records arrive structured and parsed
- **Biometric pre-collection prompts** so patients arrive with relevant home measurements ready
- **n8n parses and structures all submissions** into a clean clinician-facing brief — your doctor walks into the 1-hour consultation with data already organized
- **Reclaims 15–25 minutes per consultation** currently lost to in-room intake

**Expected outcome:** Your 1-hour consultations become 1 hour of clinical value, not 20 minutes of admin.

---

### Workflow 3: Longitudinal Follow-Up Engine
**Fixes: Communication Failures**

- **Automated Evaluation & Review cadence** — n8n triggers check-ins at clinically appropriate intervals post-treatment (Week 2, Week 6, Month 3, etc.) based on each patient's treatment plan
- **Two-way WhatsApp/SMS response capture** — patients reply with how they're feeling; responses are logged to their record and flag abnormal responses to clinical staff
- **Review solicitation automation** — triggers a Google review request 14 days post-positive-outcome (solving your cluster-of-reviews problem and building consistent social proof)
- **Re-engagement sequences** for patients who miss a follow-up or fall out of the Evaluation & Review loop — the exact moment chronic-care patients are silently lost
- **Staff dashboard** showing which patients are due for review, which have gone quiet, and which need clinical attention

**Expected outcome:** The 4-step methodology becomes an actual system, not a description.
