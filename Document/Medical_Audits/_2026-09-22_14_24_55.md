### **Administrative Gaps:** ⚠️ **CONFIRMED — HIGH SEVERITY**

**Evidence from Scrape:**

The website proudly outlines a **4-Step Patient Management Process**:

> **01 — Data Gathering:** *"We go deep – a detailed patient intake questionnaire, biometric measurements, and a review (if applicable) of past patient medical records, and blood tests."*

**Critical Gap:** There is **zero mention** of:
- A digital intake form link sent post-booking
- A patient portal for uploading past medical records
- Pre-consultation digital questionnaire delivery

The language implies the intake questionnaire is part of the in-person or pre-visit process, but **no mechanism is described for how this is delivered**. Given the broken booking widget, it is highly probable that intake is handled via:
- Paper forms upon physical arrival, OR
- Manual email/WhatsApp PDFs sent reactively by staff

**Operational Impact:**
- **Duplicated Data Entry:** Staff manually transcribe patient-provided information into EMR systems.
- **Extended On-Site Time:** Patients complete tedious paperwork during their 1+ hour consultation window—time that should be dedicated to clinical care.
- **No Proactive Intake Trigger:** Without an automated post-booking intake sequence, patients arrive unprepared, delaying the diagnostic process.
- **Medical Record Review Delays:** No structured digital pipeline for patients to upload prior blood tests or records means doctors review incomplete data, potentially requiring follow-up appointments.

**Severity Justification:** For a clinic offering "comprehensive" and "personalized" assessments, the absence of a digital intake pipeline is a direct contradiction to their brand promise. It bottlenecks clinical throughput and degrades patient experience.

### **Communication Failures:** ✅ **CONFIRMED — CRITICAL SEVERITY**

**Evidence from Scrape:**

**1. Weekend & After-Hours Vulnerability:**
> *"Opening Hours: Monday: 9:00AM – 1:00PM; Tuesday – Saturday: 9:00AM – 5:00PM; Sunday: CLOSED"*

- **Sunday:** Fully closed.
- **Monday:** Only 4 hours (9AM–1PM).
- **Tuesday–Saturday:** 8 hours.

This means the clinic is **unreachable for 118+ hours per week** (nights + Sundays + Monday afternoons). During these windows:
- WhatsApp messages go unanswered.
- Phone calls are missed.
- Website booking is broken.
- **Zero automated capture** of leads.

**2. No Automated Appointment Reminders Mentioned:**
Despite a multi-step patient journey (Data Gathering → Consultation → Treatment → Evaluation & Review), there is **no evidence** of:
- Automated SMS/WhatsApp appointment reminders
- Post-consultation follow-up sequences
- Structured retention loops (e.g., "Your test results are ready," "Time for your review appointment")

**3. Patient Reviews Reveal Follow-Up Dependency:**
Sarahbasir's review states:
> *"Looking forward to working with her again. See you again at my next appointment!"*

This implies the patient is **self-managing their follow-up**. Without automated nudges, patients may forget to book their evaluation (Step 04), leading to **treatment abandonment** and revenue loss.

**Operational Impact:**
- **Lead Leakage:** Weekend/evening inquiries are lost to competitors with 24/7 automated booking.
- **No-Show Rates:** Without reminders, no-shows increase operational waste.
- **Retention Erosion:** Patients complete initial consultations but drop off before the high-value "Evaluation & Review" phase—the retention loop is broken.
- **Reactive Staff:** Team spends time chasing leads and rescheduling instead of proactive care coordination.

**Severity Justification:** The clinic's core value proposition is a **longitudinal, 4-step process**. Without automated communication triggers at each step, the entire model is vulnerable to patient drop-off. The 118-hour weekly communication blackout is a **revenue hemorrhage**.
