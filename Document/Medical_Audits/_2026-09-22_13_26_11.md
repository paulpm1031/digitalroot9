# Operational Bottleneck Analysis: The Rosewood Clinic

## Key Findings from Website Data

**1. Booking Friction — CONFIRMED (Severe)**
The entire "Make an appointment" section is a Cloudflare Turnstile CAPTCHA widget showing repeated failure states embedded in the page text: *"Checking your Browser… Verifying... Stuck? Verification failed... Verification expired... Refresh"* — This is a **single point of failure sitting directly between an interested patient and a booked appointment.** Patients who fail verification (common on mobile, corporate VPNs, older browsers) hit a dead end with no fallback booking path. Additionally, the booking flow requires patients to select from a **7-doctor dropdown with no availability calendar, no consultation type selection, and no pricing guidance** — driving uncertainty and abandonment.

**2. Administrative Gaps — CONFIRMED**
Step 01 of their own 4-step process is *"a detailed patient intake questionnaire, biometric measurements, and a review of past patient medical records, and blood tests."* There is **no digital intake link, no patient portal, no pre-consultation form visible anywhere** on the site. This implies intake happens manually — by phone, PDF, or paper at the clinic — consuming staff time and delaying the 1+ hour consultation they pride themselves on.

**3. Communication Failures — PARTIALLY CONFIRMED (Hidden Risk)**
Reviews are uniformly glowing, which is actually a **warning sign of selection bias** — unhappy patients who couldn't book or never got a follow-up don't leave reviews. The 4-step process culminates in *"Evaluation & Review: We monitor, review, and optimise"* — but there is **no visible mechanism (no portal, no automated check-in, no recall system)** supporting this promise at scale. Every review praises *individual* doctor attentiveness, meaning follow-up depends entirely on human memory rather than systems.

**Critical structural bottleneck:** Opening hours are **9AM–1PM Monday only**, 9–5 Tue–Sat, **closed Sunday entirely**. Working professionals in KL cannot easily call during business hours — making the broken online booking even more damaging.
