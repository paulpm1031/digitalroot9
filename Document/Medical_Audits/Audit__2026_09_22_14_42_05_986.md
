## 1. Key Findings

### **Booking Friction: 🔴 CRITICAL (Confirmed)**

The scrape reveals a **hard failure point embedded directly in the conversion path**:

> *"Select Doctor — Dr Iman Thalia / Dr Iman Lim / Dr Krishnaveni Kanason / Dr Jevan Gunasenalan / Professor Rahmattulah Khan / Shelagh MacFarlane / Dr Lim Kheng Yee"*
>
> *"Checking your Browser… Verifying... Stuck? Troubleshoot. **Verification failed. Verification expired. Refresh.**"*
>
> *"— Cloudflare Turnstile challenge —"*

**Evidence of active leakage:**

1. **Broken Turnstile CAPTCHA is visible on the live booking form.** The scrape literally captured the failure state: *"Verification failed," "Verification expired,"* and repeated *"Refresh"* prompts. This means real prospective patients are hitting this wall **right now** and abandoning the booking. This is the single most costly bottleneck on the page — it sits at the exact moment of highest purchase intent.
2. **No real-time calendar or slot availability is exposed.** After selecting a doctor, the visitor is not shown bookable time slots in the scrape. There is no evidence of an instant digital scheduling layer (Calendly/Acuity-style or custom). This pushes patients toward a *"call or WhatsApp us and wait"* model — a manual, high-friction conversion path.
3. **Contact is phone + WhatsApp only** (*"+60 3 4810 6037 / WhatsApp: +60 13 575 8121"*), with no self-serve booking confirmation flow. For a KL functional-medicine audience accustomed to instant digital booking, this is a conversion killer.
4. **7-doctor selection dropdown with no guidance.** A first-time patient with no brand familiarity has to self-triage across seven practitioners with zero matching logic — decision paralysis at the point of booking.

**Severity justification:** The Turnstile error is not a theoretical risk — it is a *confirmed, reproduced failure* in the scraped data. Every patient who hits it and doesn't refresh (most won't) is a lost booking.

---

### **Administrative Gaps: 🟠 HIGH (Confirmed)**

The clinic's own published **4-step process** exposes a manual-burden workflow:

> *"01 **Data Gathering** — We go deep – a detailed patient intake questionnaire, biometric measurements, and a review of past patient medical records and blood tests."*

**Evidence of friction:**

1. **The "detailed patient intake questionnaire" is described as Step 1 of an in-clinic/direct process — with no evidence of a proactive digital intake link sent immediately post-booking.** Best-practice intake should arrive *in the patient's inbox/WhatsApp within 60 seconds of booking* — not be discovered on arrival.
2. **No mention of any pre-consultation digital form delivery mechanism.** There is no "You'll receive your intake form via email/WhatsApp" reassurance on the page — a missing trust signal and a missing automation trigger.
3. **Manual retrieval of past medical records and blood tests** ("a review if applicable of past patient medical records") implies staff-side manual chasing — duplicated data entry and admin time per patient.
4. **The 1+ hour consultation** (a premium, high-value appointment) is *wasted on data capture* if the intake hasn't been completed beforehand — the doctor's most expensive hour is being spent on admin that a pre-consult form would have solved.

**Severity justification:** Every manual intake = (a) longer front-desk queues, (b) duplicated data entry, (c) reduced doctor capacity per day, and (d) lower perceived professionalism versus digital-first competitors.

---

### **Communication Failures: 🟠 HIGH (Confirmed)**

**Evidence from operational structure + review patterns:**

1. **Weekend/after-hours black hole:** *"Sunday: CLOSED."* and *"Monday: 9:00AM – 1:00PM"* (half-day). Any lead who submits an enquiry Friday evening through Sunday night receives **zero automated response** until Monday. In the high-intent functional-medicine space, a 48-hour silence = a booking with a competitor.
2. **Zero evidence of automated appointment reminders.** No mention anywhere of SMS/WhatsApp reminders, pre-visit prep instructions, or "reply Y to confirm" flows — which are standard churn-reducers for a 1+ hour, premium-priced consultation.
3. **No structured post-consultation retention loop is visible.** The 4-step process ends at *"04 Evaluation & Review,"* but there is no automated trigger described to *bring the patient back* for that review. Retention currently depends on the patient remembering to rebook — a known dropout point.
4. **Reviews confirm the *human* touch is excellent** (*"husna!", "Dr Deanna was warm… took a lot of time to explain"*) but **all of it is manual and unscalable.** The clinic's strength (personal care) is bottlenecked by the absence of automation holding the *logistical* layer.

**Severity justification:** After-hours leads + no reminders + no retention triggers = the classic "leaky bucket." The clinic is paying (via ads/reviews) to acquire patients it then drops through silence.
