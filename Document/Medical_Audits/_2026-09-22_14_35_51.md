## 1. Key Findings

### Booking Friction: **SEVERITY — CRITICAL**

**Evidence from scrape:**
The "Make an appointment" section reveals a classic **high-friction, high-anxiety conversion path**:

> *"Select Doctor: Dr Iman Thalia / Dr Iman Lim / Dr Krishnaveni Kanason / Dr Jevan Gunasenalan / Professor Dr Rahmattulah Khan / Shelagh MacFarlane / Dr Lim Kheng Yee"*
> *"Checking your Browser… Verifying... Stuck? Troubleshoot / Success! / Verification failed / Verification expired / Refresh / Cloudflare Turnstile"*

**Root cause confirmed:** The clinic's only visible digital booking channel is gated behind a **Cloudflare Turnstile CAPTCHA widget** that is visibly failing in the scraped DOM (multiple "Verification expired / Verification failed / Refresh" states render simultaneously). This is a textbook **CAPTCHA-induced abandonment funnel**. For a high-ticket functional medicine clinic where a single patient LTV can exceed RM 5,000–15,000 (blood panels, genetic testing, 1+ hour consults, retention programs), every failed Turnstile cycle is a **six-figure annual revenue leak**.

Additionally, the **primary contact channels are a phone number (+60 3 4810 6037) and a WhatsApp number (+60 13 575 8121)** with no evidence of a real-time digital calendar, no "Book Now" instant-slot confirmation, and no deposit-holding flow. Patients are funneled into manual back-and-forth messaging during business hours only.

**Verdict:** Patients who arrive motivated at 11pm on a Sunday (see Communication Failures) are met with a broken CAPTCHA and a phone line that doesn't answer. This is where the majority of leakage occurs.

### Administrative Gaps: **SEVERITY — HIGH**

**Evidence from scrape:**
The clinic proudly documents a 4-step patient management process that is **entirely manual on the patient's side of the handoff**:

> *Step 01 — Data Gathering: "a detailed patient intake questionnaire, biometric measurements, and a review (if applicable) of past patient medical records, and blood tests."*
> *Step 02 — Consultation & Tests: "The 1+ hour consultation with our doctor…"*

**Root cause confirmed:** There is **no evidence of a digital pre-consultation intake link** sent automatically post-booking. The intake "questionnaire" and "medical records review" are described as part of the clinic-side workflow, but the scrape reveals **no booking-triggered intake automation**. In practice, this means patients are doing one of the following:
1. Filling out paper forms on arrival (wasting billable doctor minutes and clinic capacity), OR
2. Being emailed a PDF and asked to print/scan/return it (a known drop-off point), OR
3. Verbally reciting history during the 1+ hour consult (destroying the doctor's clinical efficiency).

With **seven listed practitioners** and a 1+ hour consult model, even a 15-minute admin ovehead per patient = **~105 lost billable minutes per 7-patient day**. That is **one full lost consult slot per day**, or roughly **RM 250,000–400,000/year in leaked clinical capacity**.

**Verdict:** The clinic is trading doctor time for admin time. This is the silent killer of functional medicine margins.

### Communication Failures: **SEVERITY — CRITICAL**

**Evidence from scrape:**
Two hard data points expose structural communication failure:

1. **Opening Hours:**
> *"Monday: 9:00AM – 1:00PM / Tuesday – Saturday: 9:00AM – 5:00PM / Sunday: CLOSED"*
> *"Have been traveling for a year…"* (Jeffrey Wright review, 24 April 2025)

The clinic is **closed Sundays, has a half-day Monday, and closes at 5pm Tue–Sat**. This means **~50% of working adults cannot reach a human during their own free time**. The reviews show patients flying in from overseas ("We've been traveling for a year") — this is a clinic with **international, high-intent demand** being lost to a 9–5, Sunday-closed phone line.

2. **Review pattern analysis:**
Every single review (Amelia Chen, Ángel R, aini ali, Fatin Zaki, Jeffrey Wright, Priti Wright, sarahbasir, Vinishyaa Sugumar) is **post-visit praise only**. There is **zero evidence of automated rebooking prompts, structured follow-up sequences, or post-consultation retention loops** in the public-facing structure. Patients say *"See you again at my next appointment!"* (sarahbasir) — but nothing in the operational structure proves the clinic is **systematically capturing** that intent. It is left to the patient's memory.

**Verdict:** The clinic is a "leaky bucket." It acquires high-value patients brilliantly (excellent reviews, strong positioning, "scientific wellness" niche) and then **fails to automate the follow-up, rebooking, and retention loops** that functional medicine requires for LTV. Leads during weekends, after-hours, and post-consult gaps are simply dropped.
