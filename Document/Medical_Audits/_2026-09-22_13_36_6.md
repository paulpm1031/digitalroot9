# Operational Bottleneck Analysis: The Rosewood Clinic

## Key Findings

### 1. Booking Friction (Severe)
- **No online booking system exists.** The "Make an appointment" section is a bare form asking patients to *manually select a doctor from a dropdown* — with no visibility into availability, no calendar, no confirmation flow.
- The only direct channels offered are **phone (+60 3 4810 6037)** and **WhatsApp (+60 13 575 8121)** — meaning every booking during working hours requires staff intervention.
- **A Cloudflare Turnstile challenge sits directly on the booking form.** Patients must pass a bot-verification wall just to request an appointment. On slow connections or older devices (common among the clinic's chronic-disease demographic), this causes abandoned bookings.
- **Sunday closure + weekday-only hours (9AM–5PM, Mon until 1PM)** means the clinic's working population of patients can only book while they themselves are at work.

### 2. Administrative Gaps (Severe)
- The clinic's own 4-step process states Step 1 is *"a detailed patient intake questionnaire, biometric measurements, and a review of past patient medical records."* **No digital intake link is provided anywhere on the site.** New patients almost certainly fill this out on paper or via back-and-forth WhatsApp messages.
- **No mention of a patient portal**, record upload, or digital consent forms — despite the clinic collecting genetic data, bloodwork, and biomarker panels (highly sensitive data requiring structured intake).
- Multi-doctor practice (7 practitioners listed) with **no per-doctor scheduling logic**, meaning front-desk staff manually route patients — a classic source of double-bookings and delays.

### 3. Communication Failures (Moderate — symptom of manual ops)
- Reviews are glowing on *clinical care* (Dr. Deanna, Dr. Krish, Dr. Iman are repeatedly praised for **time and thoroughness**) — but this strength is precisely what strains the system. The 1+ hour consults that patients love mean **fewer slots and higher rebooking demand**, all handled manually.
- Multiple reviewers explicitly say *"Looking forward to working with her again"* and *"See you again at my next appointment"* — **repeat-visit intent is high, but there is no automated recall, reminder, or rebooking mechanism visible.** Follow-up depends on the patient chasing the clinic.
- No automated post-visit check-in, review request, or treatment-plan adherence messaging — the entire "Evaluation & Review" step (Step 4) appears to rely on manual outreach.

**Net effect:** The clinic's clinical excellence is being throttled by a manual front desk. Patients who are ready to book, ready to return, and ready to refer are being asked to jump through phone calls, WhatsApp threads, and CAPTCHA walls — and the ones who don't bother become silent churn.
