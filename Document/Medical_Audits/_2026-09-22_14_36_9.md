# Operational Bottleneck Analysis: The Rosewood Clinic

## Key Findings

### 1. Booking Friction — **Critical**
- Appointment form terminates in a **Cloudflare Turnstile CAPTCHA loop** ("Verifying... / Verification failed / Verification expired"). Patients who fail bot verification are bounced with no fallback — every failed verification is a lost booking.
- Doctor selection is a **manual dropdown** with 7 practitioners and no visibility into availability, specialties, or real-time slots.
- No self-service calendar; after submitting the form, patients must wait for a manual callback. For a clinic whose core selling point is a **1+ hour consultation**, friction at the booking step is especially costly.
- No online rescheduling or cancellation path — all changes route through phone/WhatsApp during limited hours (**Mon 9–1 only**, closed Sunday).

### 2. Administrative Gaps — **High**
- Step 01 of their own 4-step process promises a "detailed patient intake questionnaire," but no digital intake form exists anywhere on the site. This is almost certainly handled via paper or PDF email — slow, error-prone, and it delays the doctor's prep for time-intensive consults.
- No visible upload channel for "past patient medical records and blood tests," despite the site explicitly promising to review them.
- CAPTCHA failure = zero lead capture: no email fallback, no "we'll call you" safety net, no abandonment recovery.

### 3. Communication Failures — **Moderate (latent)**
- Reviews are overwhelmingly positive, but they reveal a **single point of failure**: patients repeatedly name individual staff ("especially Husna," Dr Deanna, Dr Krish). This signals that follow-up quality depends on individual heroics, not systems.
- No automated appointment reminders, pre-consult prep instructions, or post-consult follow-up sequences are evident.
- International patients (e.g., the Wrights, traveling for a year) have no automated onboarding path across time zones.

**Bottom line:** The clinic delivers a premium in-person experience but its digital front door is failing it — a fragile CAPTCHA gate, manual booking, and paper-based intake directly undercut the "sleek, modern, professional" brand reviewers praise.
