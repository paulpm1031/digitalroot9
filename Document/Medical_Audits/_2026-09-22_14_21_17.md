# Operational Bottleneck Analysis: The Rosewood Clinic

## 1. Booking Friction (Severe)

The booking section exposes **seven doctors in a dropdown** but provides **no calendar, no real-time availability, and no self-service confirmation**. Combined with a **Cloudflare Turnstile CAPTCHA** that generates "Verification failed / expired / Stuck?" error states, the highest-intent moment in the funnel (a patient ready to book) is gated behind a challenge loop. There is **no WhatsApp booking link** despite advertising a WhatsApp number, and **no online scheduling portal** — patients are implicitly funnelled into calling during limited hours (Mon 9–1 only; closed Sundays). A functional medicine consult is a considered, high-ticket decision; forcing a phone call loses the after-hours researcher.

## 2. Administrative Gaps (Moderate–High)

Step 01 "Data Gathering" describes a **"detailed patient intake questionnaire"** and **"review of past medical records, and blood tests"** with no digital intake link, upload portal, or pre-visit form mentioned. Every review praises the **1+ hour consultation** — meaning patient history is likely collected *during* that paid clinical hour, burning doctor time on admin and capping daily patient throughput. There is no visible mechanism for records/blood test uploads before arrival.

## 3. Communication Failures (Latent but Evidenced)

Reviews repeatedly praise staff and doctors but describe **first consultations only** ("Looking forward to working with her again," "See you again at my next appointment"). The 4-step process promises **"monitor, review, and optimise"** — yet nothing in the text demonstrates automated follow-up, appointment reminders, or check-in sequences. In a protocol-driven clinic, the gap between "treatment plan" and "review" is where patients silently drop off.
