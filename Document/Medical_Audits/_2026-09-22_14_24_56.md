## 1. Key Findings

### **Booking Friction: 🔴 CONFIRMED — HIGH SEVERITY**

The scrape captured **direct evidence of a broken conversion path** at the most critical moment in the patient journey — the "Make an appointment" section.

**Specific evidence:**
- The booking widget is gated behind a **Cloudflare Turnstile CAPTCHA challenge**, and the scrape caught it in a *failure state*. The raw HTML contains:
  - `Checking your Browser…` → `Verifying...` → `Stuck?`
  - `Verification failed`
  - `Verification expired` (appearing **twice**, requiring manual refresh)
- Critically, the only conversion block on the page is a **"Select Doctor" dropdown** (Dr Iman Thalia, Dr Iman Lim, Dr Krishnaveni Kanason, Dr Jevan Gunasenalan, Professor Rahmattulah Khan, Shelagh MacFarlane, Dr Lim Kheng Yee) — there is **no visible real-time calendar, no slot-picker, and no confirmation flow**. The user is funneled into a challenge screen rather than a schedule.
- No alternative conversion paths are surfaced prominently. The contact page link is generic; there is **no evidence of instant "Book Now" self-scheduling**.

**Operational impact:** A patient ready to book — arguably the highest-intent visitor — hits a bot-verification wall that *frequently fails*. Each failure is a silent drop-off. On mobile (the majority of KL search traffic), a multi-step Turnstile + refresh loop is a near-guaranteed abandonment event.

---

### **Administrative Gaps: 🔴 CONFIRMED — HIGH SEVERITY**

The clinic openly describes a **heavy manual "Data Gathering" phase** as Step 01 of its 4-step process, with no digital delivery mechanism evident:

**Specific evidence:**
- Step 01 states: *"We go deep – a detailed patient intake questionnaire, biometric measurements, and a review (if applicable) of past patient medical records, and blood tests."*
- The phrase **"a detailed patient intake questionnaire"** combined with **biometric measurements taken in-clinic** strongly indicates a **paper/form-on-arrival intake model**. There is **no mention of a pre-consultation digital intake link** sent post-booking anywhere on the page.
- The 1+ hour consultation (Step 02) is a premium, high-value clinical asset — yet the "Data Gathering" step is not automated, meaning **clinician time is likely being spent chasing or transcribing intake data** rather than on diagnosis.

**Operational impact:** Patients arrive, fill out lengthy forms, and clinicians manually reconcile records. This delays the start of the 1-hour consult, compresses billable clinical time, and creates data-entry error risk — a direct threat to the *"thorough"* experience reviewers repeatedly praise (Jeffrey Wright: *"comprehensive… thorough"*; Vinishyaa: *"really investigate the deeper level"*).

---

### **Communication Failures: 🟠 CONFIRMED — MODERATE-TO-HIGH SEVERITY**

There is **no evidence of any automated reminder, follow-up, or retention infrastructure** on the page — a notable omission for a clinic whose revenue model depends on **recurring review cycles**.

**Specific evidence:**
- **Opening hours reveal a hard closure gap:** *"Sunday: CLOSED"* and *"Monday: 9:00AM – 1:00PM"* only. This means **any inquiry submitted Friday after 5:00PM through Monday 9:00AM sits unanswered for up to ~64 hours.** Weekend lead drop-off is structurally guaranteed.
- **Only manual channels are listed:** Tel `+60 3 4810 6037` and WhatsApp `+60 13 575 8121`. No chatbot, no auto-responder, no booking-confirmation automation.
- **Zero retention loop is visible.** Step 04 is *"Evaluation & Review — We monitor, review, and optimise,"* yet there is no mention of automated follow-ups, re-test reminders, or care-plan check-ins. Patients like *sarahbasir* openly state *"See you again at my next appointment!"* — demand for retention exists, but the **system to trigger those recalls is absent.**
- Reviews are uniformly glowing — meaning the **clinical product is excellent and the operational layer is the weak link.** That is the most fixable, highest-ROI scenario for automation.

**Operational impact:** Leads die over weekends. Patients forget re-test windows. High-LTV functional medicine patients (genetic testing, metabolic panels, ongoing protocols) churn simply because **nobody is triggered to bring them back.**
