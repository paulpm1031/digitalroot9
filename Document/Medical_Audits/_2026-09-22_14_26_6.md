# Operational Bottleneck Analysis: The Rosewood Clinic

## Key Findings from Text Data

**1. Booking Friction — CONFIRMED**
- The "Make an appointment" section is a single dropdown (Select Doctor) with **no visible digital scheduling flow** (no calendar, no time-slot selection, no online confirmation).
- A **Cloudflare Turnstile CAPTCHA** gates the form. The rendered states visible in the text — *"Checking your Browser… Verifying… Verification failed / Verification expired / Stuck? Troubleshoot"* — indicate the booking widget is fragile. Patients who fail verification are shown a **troubleshoot link to Cloudflare, not to the clinic** — a dead end that silently kills bookings.
- No self-serve rescheduling, no waitlist, no instant confirmation mentioned anywhere.

**2. Administrative Gaps — CONFIRMED**
- Step 01 of their own 4-step process is "a **detailed patient intake questionnaire**, biometric measurements, and a review of past patient medical records, and blood tests." This is described as manual, front-loaded data gathering — the classic paper/PDF intake pattern. No digital intake link, portal, or pre-visit form is referenced.
- Six practitioners are listed with no routing logic shown — intake data isn't being triaged to the right doctor automatically.

**3. Communication Failures — PARTIAL RISK**
- Reviews are overwhelmingly positive on in-room care, but note the pattern: *"See you again at my next appointment!"* and *"Looking forward to working with her again"* — **no review mentions reminders, follow-ups, or between-visit communication.**
- Their Step 04 ("We monitor, review, and optimise") implies ongoing follow-up, but **nothing in the text shows an automated recall, check-in, or results-follow-up mechanism.** This is where patients quietly churn after visit one.
