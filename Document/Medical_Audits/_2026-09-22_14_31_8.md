**2. Administrative Gaps — HIGH**

- No booking confirmation/pre-visit automation is evident. A 1+ hour consultation model with heavy pre-visit data gathering means every manual form = staff hours + no-shows + patients arriving unprepared.

**3. Communication Failures — MODERATE (latent)**
- Reviews are glowing on *in-consultation* care (Dr Deanna, Dr Krish, Dr Iman, Husna all named), but there is **zero evidence of any post-visit follow-up system**: no recall reminders, no re-test nudges for their "Evaluation & Review" step, no review-request automation (despite an impressive organic review stream).
