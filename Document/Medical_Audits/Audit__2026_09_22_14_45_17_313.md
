## 1. Key Findings

### **Booking Friction: CONFIRMED — SEVERE**

The scrape reveals a **high-friction, fragile booking path** that is actively bleeding conversions at the critical moment of intent.

**Evidence:**
- **Cloudflare Turnstile is visibly failing/looping on the live booking widget.** The raw capture shows the full failure cascade rendered into the DOM: *"Checking your Browser… Verifying... Stuck? Verification failed / Verification expired / Refresh."* When a bot-check UI is this deep in the DOM tree (`challenges.cloudflare.com/cdn-cgi/challenge-platform/...`), it means the widget repeatedly re-issued challenges to real users. Every "Verification expired" is a **human being who tried to book and walked away.**
- **No real-time calendar or slot picker.** The booking flow forces a doctor dropdown (*Select Doctor → Dr Iman Thalia, Dr Krishnaveni, Dr Jevan, etc.*) with **no visible date/time availability**. Patients cannot self-serve around their own schedules — a fatal flaw for a KL-based clinic serving busy working professionals.
- **Manual channels are the fallback.** Only a phone number (`+60 3 4810 6037`) and a WhatsApp line (`+60 13 575 8121`) are exposed as contact points — both requiring human response during business hours.
- **The 1+ hour consultation is high-value, low-friction-tolerance.** Functional medicine consultations at this tier typically sit in the RM 400–800+ range. Abandonment at the Turnstile directly costs hundreds of ringgit per lost lead.

**Diagnosis:** The booking flow is a *leaky bucket*. Marketing spend, Google reviews, and referrals are pushing warm traffic into a form that fails before intent converts.

---

### **Administrative Gaps: CONFIRMED — SEVERE**

The clinic publicly advertises a **4-step patient management process** that is fundamentally *manual paperflow disguised as methodology*.

**Evidence (Step 01: Data Gathering):**
> *"We go deep – a detailed patient intake questionnaire, biometric measurements, and a review (if applicable) of past patient medical records, and blood tests."*

- **The "detailed patient intake questionnaire" is the red flag.** The clinic's own copy describes intake as a *first-step activity* — not a pre-arrival digital process. In operation, this almost certainly means: patient arrives → sits in waiting room → fills out a paper or tablet form for 15–30 minutes → *then* the 1+ hour paid consultation begins.
- **This is duplicated effort and wasted billable time.** Every minute a patient spends filling out history in the waiting room is a minute the doctor could have used for diagnosis. For a 1-hour consult, a 20-minute intake burn = **~33% of the paid session wasted on data entry.**
- **No mention of any pre-consultation digital intake link, patient portal, or automated form dispatch.** No confirmation-of-booking email → intake form automation is described anywhere on the site.
- **"Review of past medical records and blood tests" is a manual chase.** No upload portal is referenced. This means staff are likely requesting records via WhatsApp/email and manually reconciling PDFs into the patient file.

**Diagnosis:** This is textbook **Lean waste — Defects (incomplete intake), Waiting (patient in lobby), Over-processing (staff re-keying data)**. It caps the clinic's throughput and adds clinical risk on the day of consult.

---

### **Communication Failures: CONFIRMED — MODERATE-TO-SEVERE**

The clinic's reviews are glowing — but the *operations behind them* show no evidence of automated retention infrastructure.

**Evidence:**
- **Opening hours expose a 16-hour overnight + full Sunday blackout.** *"Sunday: CLOSED. Monday: 9:00AM – 1:00PM only."* Any WhatsApp or web inquiry submitted Sunday or Monday afternoon sits unanswered into Tuesday. In functional medicine — where patients are often anxious, researching at night, and comparing 3–4 clinics — **a 24–48 hour response gap is a lost booking.**
- **Reviews confirm a strong follow-up *human* culture but no *systemic* loop.** Multiple testimonials say *"See you again at my next appointment!"* and *"Looking forward to working with her again"* — yet there is **zero visible mechanism** driving that return: no automated post-consult review request, no structured 30/60/90-day check-in trigger, no retention sequence for the chronic-disease patients (diabetes, hypertension, obesity) who *need* recurring touchpoints.
- **No automated reminder evidence.** For a clinic where a no-show costs an hour of specialist time, the absence of any visible SMS/WhatsApp reminder cadence is a material capacity risk.
- **The "Evaluation & Review" step (Step 04) is described as a promise, not a system.** *"We monitor, review, and optimise"* — with no automation behind it, this is manually driven by busy staff and will silently fail as patient volume grows.

**Diagnosis:** The clinic has **no structured post-consultation retention loop**, no after-hours lead capture, and no automated reminder cadence. Every returning patient is currently a *coincidence of good service*, not a *designed outcome*.
