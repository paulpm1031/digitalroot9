# Operational Bottleneck Analysis: The Rosewood Clinic

## Key Findings

**1. Booking Friction — SEVERE**
The "Make an appointment" section requires patients to manually select from a dropdown of 7 doctors, then immediately hits a **Cloudflare Turnstile CAPTCHA** ("Checking your Browser… Verifying…"). Critically, there is **no online self-scheduling calendar** — no visible time slots, no confirmation flow shown. Patients must either tolerate a clunky web form or fall back to phone/WhatsApp (+60 3 4810 6037 / +60 13 575 8121) during limited hours (closed Sundays; Mon only 9AM–1PM). For a clinic whose reviews repeatedly praise its modern, holistic positioning, the booking experience contradicts the brand.

**2. Administrative Gaps — HIGH**
Step 01 of their own 4-step process ("Data Gathering") explicitly relies on a *"detailed patient intake questionnaire"* — but nothing on the page indicates a digital intake link. Combined with a 1+ hour consultation model and "review of past patient medical records and blood tests," this strongly suggests paper forms or manual uploads. This creates a bottlenecks at check-in and eats into the very consultation time patients are paying premium rates for.

**3. Communication Failures — MODERATE (latent)**
Reviews mention *"See you again at my next appointment"* and *"Looking forward to working with her again"* — implying a recall/rebooking cycle exists. But there is no visible automated reminder, post-consultation follow-up, or recall system. In functional medicine, where treatment plans span months ("monitor, review, and optimise"), **silent drop-off between appointments is the #1 revenue and retention killer**.
