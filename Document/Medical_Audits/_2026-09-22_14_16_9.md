# Operational Bottleneck Analysis: The Rosewood Clinic

## Diagnosis of the 3 Core Process Challenges

### 1. Booking Friction — **CRITICAL**
The "Make an appointment" section reveals a fatal conversion killer:
- The booking form is gated behind a **Cloudflare Turnstile human-verification challenge** that shows seven different failure states — "Checking your Browser…", "Verifying...", "Stuck?", "Verification failed", "Verification expired", "Refresh", "Troubleshoot."
- For a high-intent patient researching functional medicine (often unwell, fatigued, or anxious — the exact audience listed: *"exhaustion, fatigue, stress, anxiety"*), an ambiguous CAPTCHA wall is where bookings die.
- A doctor **dropdown selector with 7 practitioners** (Dr Iman Thalia, Dr Iman Lim, Dr Krishnaveni, Dr Jevan, Prof. Rahmattulah, Shelagh MacFarlane, Dr Lim Kheng Yee) implies manual routing — no smart triage, no availability calendar, no instant confirmation.
- The primary "Contact Us" CTA links to a generic contact page, not a booking flow. **No digital scheduler exists.** Patients must call (+60 3 4810 6037) or WhatsApp (+60 13 575 8121) during restrictive hours — **Sunday closed, Monday only 9AM–1PM**.

### 2. Administrative Gaps — **HIGH**
The clinic proudly describes Step 01 of its 4-step process as: *"a detailed patient intake questionnaire, biometric measurements, and a review of past patient medical records, and blood tests."*
- There is **no visible digital intake link, no patient portal, no pre-consultation form** mentioned anywhere on the page. For a clinic selling a "1+ hour consultation" that depends on deep data gathering, this intake is almost certainly happening via paper forms or clipboard-in-waiting-room — eating into the paid consultation hour and creating data-entry errors.
- "Review of past patient medical records" implies manual chasing of documents via phone/email — a classic administrative black hole.

### 3. Communication Failures — **MODERATE (Emerging Risk)**
Reviews are currently glowing (Amelia, Ángel, aini ali, Fatin, the Wrights, sarahbasir, Vinishyaa), but scrutiny reveals the risk:
- **Every single review mentions the individual doctor by name** ("Dr Deanna," "Dr Krish," "Dr Iman") — the clinic's reputation is **personality-dependent, not system-dependent**. If Dr Deanna leaves, the conversion engine leaves with her.
- Sarahbasir says: *"See you again at my next appointment!"* — but there is **no automated recall, reminder, or rebooking mechanism visible**. Follow-up ("Step 04: Evaluation & Review — monitor, review, optimise") is promised but no automated cadence exists to deliver it.
- The WhatsApp number is a manual inbox — no auto-triage, no appointment reminders, no post-visit check-ins beyond whatever a human remembers to send.

## Our Proposed n8n Automation Stack

### Fix 1 — Frictionless Booking Engine
**Replaces:** Turnstile-gated form + manual phone/WhatsApp triage

- **24/7 self-service scheduling** via n8n + Cal.com/Calendly integration, synced to each of the 7 doctors' real calendars.
- **Smart triage logic**: patient selects a concern (hormones, gut, metabolic, etc.) → n8n routes them to the right practitioner automatically, killing the "pick a doctor you've never heard of" drop-off.
- Invisible bot protection (honeypot + rate-limiting) replaces the patient-facing CAPTCHA gauntlet.
- Instant confirmation via **WhatsApp + email**, with calendar invite and pre-visit instructions.

### Fix 2 — Digital Intake & Records Pipeline
**Replaces:** Paper intake questionnaires + manual record-chasing

- Automated **digital intake form** sent the moment a booking is confirmed — detailed history, symptoms, medications, lifestyle — completed before the patient arrives.
- n8n **auto-parses submissions** into your practice management system/Google Sheets/EMR, flagging red-flag symptoms for the doctor's pre-read.
- **Document request automation**: n8n emails past providers for records on the patient's behalf, tracks responses, and escalates non-replies — no staff chasing.
- Result: the paid "1+ hour consultation" starts with data on screen, not a clipboard.

### Fix 3 — Lifecycle Communication & Recall Automation
**Replaces:** Manual WhatsApp replies + memory-dependent follow-ups

- **Automated reminder cadence**: 7-day, 48-hour, and 2-hour reminders via WhatsApp/SMS — cutting no-shows that currently cost you entire consultation slots.
- **Post-visit follow-up sequences** tied to each doctor's treatment plan ("Step 04: monitor, review, optimise" — now actually automated).
- **Recall engine**: patients due for repeat bloodwork, Wellness Panel re-tests, or follow-up consults get automatically re-engaged — turning one-time visitors into the "see you at my next appointment" recurring patients your reviews already promise.
- **Review-request automation** post-appointment to keep that 5-star pipeline flowing systematically, not luckily.
