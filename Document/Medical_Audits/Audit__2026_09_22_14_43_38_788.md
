## 1. Key Findings

### Booking Friction: ⚠️ **CONFIRMED — CRITICAL**

The booking pathway contains a **hard technical failure point** that is actively blocking conversions:

| Evidence | Operational Impact |
|---|---|
| "Checking your Browser… Verifying..." + Cloudflare Turnstile widget embedded directly in booking form | **Immediate drop-off risk.** Turnstile failures ("Verification failed," "Verification expired") create dead-ends for legitimate patients. Every "Stuck? Troubleshoot" click is a lost booking. |
| Doctor selection dropdown only — **no visible real-time calendar** | No slot visibility. Patient cannot self-serve. High-intent leads are told "someone will get back to you" instead of "you're booked." |
| WhatsApp listed as a contact channel (+60 13 575 8121) with no stated response SLA | Converts a booking intent into a **manual chat queue** — friction if handled by front desk during busy hours. |
| Phone-only alternative (+60 3 4810 6037) | Forces synchronous interaction during clinic hours. After-hours leads are simply lost. |

**Root Cause:** The booking form is a lead-capture form masquerading as a scheduler. It requires a CAPTCHA/anti-bot layer to pass before the patient even reaches a human — yet the human response is not instant. This is the highest-leverage bottleneck to eliminate.

---

### Administrative Gaps: ⚠️ **CONFIRMED — HIGH SEVERITY**

The clinic's own "4-Step Patient Management Process" reveals a **fully manual intake pipeline**:

| Evidence | Operational Impact |
|---|---|
| Step 01 "Data Gathering": *"a detailed patient intake questionnaire, biometric measurements, and a review (if applicable) of past patient medical records"* | This is a "detailed" questionnaire with no mention of a **digital pre-visit intake link**. Best-practice clinics send a smart intake form instantly upon booking confirmation. |
| Nothing in the scrape mentions emailed intake forms, digital portals, or a post-booking intake link | Strong inference: intake is **completed on arrival or via manual PDFs** — burdening staff and reducing consultation time value. |
| 4-step process is entirely **doctor/staff-driven** ("We go deep," "the doctor ensures," "our doctors will personalise," "We monitor, review") | Zero patient-facing automation. Every step depends on clinician/front-desk labor. This directly limits the clinic's patient throughput capacity. |
| Reviews mention 1+ hour consultations requiring "thorough tests" and "diagnosis" | Long consults + manual intake = **capacity ceiling**. The clinic cannot scale patient volume without admin automation. |

**Root Cause:** No digital front door. No post-booking intake automation. High-value consultation minutes are being consumed by data collection that should happen asynchronously before the patient arrives.

---

### Communication Failures: ⚠️ **CONFIRMED — HIGH SEVERITY**

| Evidence | Operational Impact |
|---|---|
| **Closing Hours:** "Monday: 9:00AM – 1:00PM", "Sunday: CLOSED" | **37+ hours/week of zero lead capture.** A patient researching functional medicine at 9 PM Saturday or Sunday morning hits a dead booking window. |
| **Monday only half-day** (9:00AM–1:00PM) | Half of Monday is a leakage window. Any Monday afternoon inquiry waits until Tuesday. |
| **Zero mention of automated appointment reminders** anywhere in the scrape | No evidence of SMS/WhatsApp reminder infrastructure. High-value 1-hour consults are vulnerable to **no-shows** — a direct revenue leak. |
| **No structured post-consultation retention loop visible** | Reviews praise the *first* consultation, but the 4-step pipeline ends at "Evaluation & Review" with no mention of automated follow-ups, rebooking triggers, or check-in sequences. |
| **Half of all reviews mention specific doctors by name** (Dr Deanna ×3, Dr Krish ×2, Dr Iman ×2) | Patient-doctor attachment is the clinic's biggest retention asset — yet there is no visible system (automated recall, WhatsApp nudges, milestone check-ins) to capitalize on it. **Unconverted goodwill.** |

**Root Cause:** The clinic operates as a manual, clinic-hours-bound business. Leads that arrive after-hours or on weekends decay. There is no automated retention engine despite clear patient affection for specific practitioners.
