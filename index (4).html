Operational Excellence Frameworks

Integrating Theory of Constraints, Lean operational waste removal, and structural Six Sigma metrics.

01

Theory of Constraints (TOC)

Resolving the definitive "Front Door" structural constraint holding back your entire organization's patient throughput.

  • Fixes insurance eligibility verification stalls
  • Eliminates paper-based or duplicate data entry
  • Prevents downstream claim friction at intake
02

Lean (DOWNTIME Mapped)

Extracting systematic operational friction across the comprehensive 8 wastes framework.

  • Defects: Billing errors and incomplete records
  • Waiting: Delays on bed slots or lab diagnostics
  • Extra-Processing: Manual re-keying across systems
03

Six Sigma (DMAIC Pass)

Targeting onboarding and billing turnaround variance to eliminate processing extremes.

  • Isolates the root 80/20 drivers of delays
  • Establishes baseline performance metrics
  • Minimizes claim lifecycle processing deviations
