Questions and answers

Questions and answers.

What will I actually get?

A clear picture of one important process in your business. Where work waits, where it is repeated, where it goes wrong, and what it costs you in time and money. You get a map of the process, the problems ranked, and one fix to do first. You decide what happens next.

Will you try to sell me software or AI?

No. We fix the process first. If a simple change fixes the problem, that is what we recommend. Software or AI comes only after the process is sound, and only if it really fixes the problem.

What do you need from us?

What you already have. How the process runs today, the records it already makes, and a short talk with the people who run it. You do not need a new system or a big clean-up project to start.

What if you find our process is fine?

Then we tell you. If the benefit is not worth further work, we explain why, instead of selling you a build you do not need. You still keep the map and the findings.

How is the fee decided?

We agree it before work starts, after we understand the process, the scope, and what you will receive. There is no package to buy, and no software you must buy just to start a review. Nothing starts until you approve it.

Can you show results from other businesses?

Not yet as a published case, and no demo number on this site is a client result. What we can show is the method: your own records, settings agreed with you in writing, and sums you can check. We only publish a real case when that client agrees in writing.

How is my business information handled?

In private. We agree what we need, who sees it, and how it is used, before we ask for it. If some information is missing, we tell you how that limits what we can conclude.
