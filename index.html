The DigitalRoot9™ Methodology

A five-stage architecture for safer operational change without displacing clinical judgement, governance, or the existing system of record.

Step 01

Diagnosis

Review approved operational data, logs, staff journeys, and baseline delays.

Step 02

Root Cause

Separate technology constraints from process, role, or workaround failures.

Step 03

Options

Generate a broad intervention set and focus on practical choices.

Step 04

Prioritise

Rank initiatives by benefit, ease, risk, and organisational readiness.

Step 05

Deploy

Pilot approved changes with testing, controls, training, and success thresholds.
